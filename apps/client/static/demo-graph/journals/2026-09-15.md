## One editor, two behaviours

This is prose: plain markdown, headings as the outline. The list below is an outliner, just by being a list.

- Tab / Shift+Tab indent and outdent, children included
  - Alt + Up / Down moves a block with everything under it
  - Click the guide line on the left to fold
- Shift+Enter continues a block; Enter splits it; Ctrl+Enter drops back to prose

## Links and pages

- `[[Double brackets]]` make a link and a concept. [[Plant]] has a page; [[Yellow Leaves]] doesn't - click it and you get a draft that only exists once you type
- [[Plants]] and [[Houseplant]] are aliases of [[Plant]]; [[Types of [[Plant]]]] is a scoped concept, filed under it
- **Backlinks**, on the right, list everything that mentions the page you're on
- Rename a page from its tab and every link is rewritten, or the old name kept as an alias

## Finding things and moving about

- **Quick Find** matches names; **Search everything** (Alt+F) adds full text
- **Favourites** are part of the graph; **Recents** are this device's
- Drag a tab to split the view or dock it to an edge. Back and Forward walk your trail, and every page reopens where you left it

## Protected documents

- [[Where I hide the good fertiliser]] is sealed. The passphrase here is `monstera`; unlock from the padlock on the left
- Unlocked, it edits like any page. It relocks by itself, is never indexed, and the key is never stored - lose the passphrase and the page is gone

## Where a real graph lives

- A **folder** on your computer: plain markdown, git-friendly, Chromium desktop only
- **Sync+**: encrypted on your device, decrypted only on your devices, with real-time co-editing and a Recovery Code that is the only way back in
- **Import** from Logseq, Obsidian or plain markdown
- This demo is neither. It lives in this browser only and **Reset demo** above puts it back. Keep anything that matters in a real graph
