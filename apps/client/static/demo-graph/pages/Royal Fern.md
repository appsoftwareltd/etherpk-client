*Osmunda regalis*, in the [[Osmundaceae]] family. Native to [[Europe]].

One of the largest native ferns in Britain, growing by streams and in bogs. Its spores grow on separate, rusty brown fronds that look like flowers, which gave it the old name flowering fern.

- Light: sun or part shade, outdoors
- Water: keep wet. It is happy at the edge of a pond

See also [[Hart's Tongue Fern]] and [[Tree Fern]].
