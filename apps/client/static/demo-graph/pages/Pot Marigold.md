*Calendula officinalis*, in the [[Asteraceae]] family. Native to the [[Mediterranean]].

```map
Andalusia, Spain @ 37.4, -4.6
Castilla-La Mancha, Spain @ 39.5, -3
Catalonia, Spain @ 41.8, 1.5
```

Not a [[French Marigold]] at all, which is a different genus. Its orange petals are edible and colour salads and rice. It seeds itself, so once grown it stays.

- Grow: sow outdoors in spring
- Flowers: early summer to the first frost

See also [[French Marigold]] and [[Nasturtium]].
