*Nematanthus gregarius*, in the [[Gesneriaceae]] family. Native to [[Brazil]].

```map
Espírito Santo, Brazil @ -19.6, -40.6
Rio de Janeiro, Brazil @ -22.41, -42.97
São Paulo, Brazil @ -23, -47.5
```

Small glossy leaves and orange flowers shaped like tiny leaping goldfish. It trails nicely from a hanging pot.

- Light: bright, indirect
- Water: when the top of the soil is dry

See also [[African Violet]].
