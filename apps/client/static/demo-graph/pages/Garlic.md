*Allium sativum*, in the [[Amaryllidaceae]] family. Native to [[Central Asia]].

```map
Almaty, Kazakhstan @ 43.2, 76.9
Kyrgyzstan @ 41.5, 74.6
Tajikistan @ 38.9, 70
Uzbekistan @ 41.3, 69.6
Turkmenistan @ 38, 57.9
```

Each clove planted grows into a whole bulb. It needs a spell of cold to split into cloves, which is why it is planted in autumn. The flower stalks of hardneck varieties, called scapes, are good to eat.

- Grow: plant cloves in autumn
- Harvest: early summer, when the lower leaves yellow

See also [[Onion]] and [[Wild Garlic]].
