*Bougainvillea glabra*, in the [[Nyctaginaceae]] family. Native to [[Brazil]].

```map
Minas Gerais, Brazil @ -18.5, -44.5
Rio de Janeiro, Brazil @ -22.41, -42.97
São Paulo, Brazil @ -23, -47.5
Paraná, Brazil @ -24.6, -51.6
```

A thorny climber covered in vivid magenta. The colour is papery bracts round tiny white flowers. In cool countries it needs a conservatory, and it flowers best when kept a little dry.

- Grow: full sun, under glass
- Flowers: summer

See also [[Rose of China]].
