*Selenicereus undatus*, in the [[Cactaceae]] family. Native to [[Mexico]] and [[Central America]].

A climbing cactus with three-sided stems and huge white flowers that open for one night only. The pink fruit follows if the flowers are pollinated. It grows easily from seed scraped out of a shop fruit.

- Grow: in a pot with a support, in a bright, warm room
- Harvest: rarely, indoors

See also [[Prickly Pear]].
