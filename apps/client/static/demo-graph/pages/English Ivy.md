*Hedera helix*, in the [[Araliaceae]] family. Native to [[Europe]].

```map
Southern England @ 51.15, -1.2
Ireland @ 53.2, -8
Central France @ 46.8, 1.7
Thuringia, Germany @ 50.9, 11
Tuscany, Italy @ 43.4, 11.1
```

An evergreen climber that clings by tiny roots. Indoors it trails from a shelf and is prone to spider mite in dry, warm air (see [[Pests]]). Outdoors its late flowers feed insects when little else is in bloom.

- Light: anything from sun to deep shade
- Water: keep slightly moist indoors

See also [[Fatsia]] and [[Golden Pothos]].
