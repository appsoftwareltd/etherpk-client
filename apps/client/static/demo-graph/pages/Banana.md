*Musa acuminata*, in the [[Musaceae]] family. Native to [[Southeast Asia]].

Not a tree. The trunk is made of tightly rolled leaf stalks, which makes the banana the largest herbaceous flowering plant there is. Dwarf varieties make big, dramatic houseplants in a bright room, though they rarely fruit indoors.

- Grow: in a big pot in a bright, warm room
- Harvest: rarely, indoors

See also [[Bird of Paradise]].
