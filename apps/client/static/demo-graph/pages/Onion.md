*Allium cepa*, in the [[Amaryllidaceae]] family. Native to [[Central Asia]].

Most gardeners grow onions from sets, small onions that are planted in spring and swell over summer. When the leaves fall over, lift the bulbs and dry them in the sun before storing. Grown near [[Carrot]], each is said to put off the other's pests.

- Grow: plant sets in spring, tips just showing
- Harvest: late summer, when the leaves topple

See also [[Garlic]], [[Chives]] and [[Carrot]].
