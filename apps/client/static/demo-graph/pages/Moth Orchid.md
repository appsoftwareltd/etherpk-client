*Phalaenopsis*, in the [[Orchidaceae]] family. Native to [[Southeast Asia]].

The supermarket orchid, and the easiest one to keep. In the wild it grows on trees, so its roots want air, not soil, and it is potted in bark. Silver roots mean it is thirsty, green roots mean it is not. After flowering, cut the stem above a node and it often flowers again from there.

- Light: bright, indirect. An east window is ideal
- Water: soak the bark when the roots turn silver, then drain

See also [[Dendrobium]] and [[Slipper Orchid]].
