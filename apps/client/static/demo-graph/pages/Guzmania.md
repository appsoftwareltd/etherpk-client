*Guzmania lingulata*, in the [[Bromeliaceae]] family. Native to [[Central America]] and [[South America]].

A rosette of glossy leaves with a star of red, orange or yellow bracts in the middle that lasts for months. Like every bromeliad, each rosette flowers only once.

- Light: bright, indirect
- Water: into the central cup, and keep the soil just moist

See also [[Urn Plant]].
