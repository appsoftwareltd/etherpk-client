*Asplenium nidus*, in the [[Aspleniaceae]] family. Native to [[Southeast Asia]], the [[Pacific Islands]] and [[Australia]].

A [[Fern]] whose leaves are not divided at all: glossy, wavy, apple-green straps around a furry centre like a nest. In the wild it grows in the forks of trees, catching falling leaves. Water around the edge, not into the middle, which rots.

- Light: medium, indirect
- Water: keep evenly moist

See also [[Hart's Tongue Fern]] and [[Boston Fern]].
