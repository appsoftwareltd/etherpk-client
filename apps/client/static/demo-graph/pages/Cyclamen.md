*Cyclamen persicum*, in the [[Primulaceae]] family. Native to the [[Mediterranean]] and [[Western Asia]].

```map
Rhodes, Greece @ 36.2, 28
Southern Turkey @ 37.1, 33
Cyprus @ 35, 33.1
Lebanon @ 33.9, 35.8
Northern Israel @ 32.9, 35.4
```

The winter houseplant with swept-back petals, like butterflies above marbled leaves. It likes it cool: a warm room shortens its life. Water from below, keeping the tuber dry, and expect it to rest in summer.

- Grow: indoors in a cool, bright room
- Flowers: autumn to spring

See also [[Primrose]].
