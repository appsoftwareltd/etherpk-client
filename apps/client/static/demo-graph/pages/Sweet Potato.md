---
*Ipomoea batatas*, in the [[Convolvulaceae]] family. Native to [[Central America]] and [[South America]].

Not related to the [[Potato]] at all. It is a morning glory, and its trailing vines have pretty heart-shaped leaves that are edible too. In cool summers it needs a greenhouse or the warmest spot you have.

- Grow: plant rooted cuttings, called slips, in early summer
- Harvest: in autumn, before the soil cools

See also [[Morning Glory]] and [[Potato]].
