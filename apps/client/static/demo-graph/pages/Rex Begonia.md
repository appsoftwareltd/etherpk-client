*Begonia rex*, in the [[Begoniaceae]] family. Native to [[India]] and [[China]].

```map
Assam, India @ 26.2, 92.9
Meghalaya, India @ 25.5, 91.4
Yunnan, China @ 25, 101.8
Guizhou, China @ 26.8, 106.8
Guangxi, China @ 23.8, 108.8
```

Grown for its leaves, which come patterned in silver, purple, pink and near-black. A single leaf, cut across its veins and pinned to damp compost, grows new plants along the cuts.

- Light: medium, indirect
- Water: when the top of the soil is dry. Keep water off the leaves

See also [[Polka Dot Begonia]].
