*Papaver rhoeas*, in the [[Papaveraceae]] family. Native to [[Europe]] and the [[Mediterranean]].

```map
Sicily, Italy @ 37.6, 14.1
Central France @ 46.8, 1.7
Tuscany, Italy @ 43.4, 11.1
Peloponnese, Greece @ 37.5, 22.3
Northern Morocco @ 35, -4.5
```

The scarlet poppy of cornfields and remembrance. Its seed can lie dormant in the soil for decades and germinate when the ground is disturbed, which is why poppies flooded the battlefields of the First World War.

- Grow: scatter seed on bare soil in autumn or spring
- Flowers: early summer
