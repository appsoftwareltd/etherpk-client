*Camellia japonica*, in the [[Theaceae]] family. Native to [[Japan]] and [[China]].

```map
Kyushu, Japan @ 32.6, 131
Shikoku, Japan @ 33.7, 133.5
Central Honshu, Japan @ 36, 138
Zhejiang, China @ 29.2, 120.1
Shandong, China @ 36.3, 118
```

Glossy evergreen leaves and perfect red, pink or white flowers in late winter. It needs acid soil, and if it goes dry in late summer it drops its buds the following spring. Its sister is the [[Tea]] plant.

- Grow: part shade, acid soil, shelter from morning sun
- Flowers: late winter to spring

See also [[Tea]] and [[Azalea]].
