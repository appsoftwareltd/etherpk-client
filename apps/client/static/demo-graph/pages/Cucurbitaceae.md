---
title: Cucurbitaceae
aliases:
  - Gourd family
  - Cucurbits
---
The gourd family: climbing or trailing plants with tendrils, and separate male and female flowers. If the fruit will not set, pollinate by hand with a small brush, from a male flower to a female one, which has a tiny fruit behind its petals.
