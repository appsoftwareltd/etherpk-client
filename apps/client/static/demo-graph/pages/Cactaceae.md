The cacti. Apart from the [[Mistletoe Cactus]], every cactus comes from the Americas. Their spines grow from small furry pads called areoles, which is what makes a cactus a cactus rather than any spiny [[Succulent]]. Not all of them live in deserts: the [[Christmas Cactus]] grows on rainforest trees.
