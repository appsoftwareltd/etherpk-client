*Platycerium bifurcatum*, in the [[Polypodiaceae]] family. Native to [[Australia]].

```map
Far North Queensland, Australia @ -17.3, 145.5
Southeast Queensland, Australia @ -27.5, 152.5
Northern New South Wales, Australia @ -29.5, 152.6
```

A [[Fern]] that grows on trees, with forked fronds like antlers and flat shield fronds that clasp the bark and collect falling debris. It is usually mounted on a board and hung on a wall.

- Light: bright, indirect
- Water: soak the mount weekly

See also [[Blue Star Fern]].
