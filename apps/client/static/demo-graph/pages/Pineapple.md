*Ananas comosus*, in the [[Bromeliaceae]] family. Native to [[South America]].

```map
Paraguay @ -25.3, -56.5
Mato Grosso, Brazil @ -13, -56
Pará, Brazil @ -4.5, -52.5
```

A bromeliad, like the [[Urn Plant]]. Twist the leafy top off a shop pineapple, dry it for a few days, then pot it, and it roots. After two or three years in a warm, bright room it may flower and grow a small fruit.

- Grow: from a pineapple top, in a warm room
- Harvest: years later, if at all

See also [[Urn Plant]] and [[Spanish Moss]].
