*Ficus microcarpa*, in the [[Moraceae]] family. Native to [[China]], [[Southeast Asia]] and [[Australia]].

```map
Guangdong, China @ 23.4, 113.5
Taiwan @ 23.7, 121
Peninsular Malaysia @ 3.8, 102.5
Java, Indonesia @ -7.3, 110
Far North Queensland, Australia @ -17.3, 145.5
```

Sold with a swollen, gnarled root at the base and a small head of leaves, often as a first bonsai. In the wild it is a banyan, dropping roots from its branches until one tree looks like a small forest.

- Light: bright, indirect
- Water: when the top of the soil is dry

See also [[Fiddle Leaf Fig]] and [[Rubber Plant]].
