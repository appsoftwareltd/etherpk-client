*Lavandula angustifolia*, in the [[Lamiaceae]] family. Native to the [[Mediterranean]].

```map
Provence, France @ 44, 5.9
Pyrenees, Spain @ 42.55, 0.6
Piedmont, Italy @ 45, 7.9
```

Called English lavender, and from the Mediterranean like the rest. It is the hardiest lavender, grown for its scent, for bees, and for little bags in drawers. Trim it every year after flowering, but never into the old bare wood, which does not regrow.

- Light: full sun
- Water: rarely, once established

See also [[Rosemary]] and [[Sage]].
