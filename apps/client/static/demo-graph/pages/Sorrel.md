*Rumex acetosa*, in the [[Polygonaceae]] family. Native to [[Europe]].

Arrow-shaped leaves with a sharp, lemony taste, for soups and sauces with fish. It is a perennial that comes up early every spring. The sourness comes from oxalic acid, as in [[Rhubarb]] leaves, so eat it in moderation.

- Light: sun or part shade
- Water: keep moist

See also [[Rhubarb]].
