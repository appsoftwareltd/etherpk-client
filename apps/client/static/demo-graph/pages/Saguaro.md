*Carnegiea gigantea*, in the [[Cactaceae]] family. Native to [[North America]] and [[Mexico]].

The tall cactus with upturned arms from every Western. It grows slowly and often takes seventy years or more to grow its first arm. It is a seedling for a long time, and seedlings are what you will find for sale.

- Light: full sun
- Water: very little

See also [[Golden Barrel Cactus]].
