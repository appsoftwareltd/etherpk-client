*Malus domestica*, in the [[Rosaceae]] family. Native to [[Central Asia]].

The wild ancestor still grows in the forests of the Tian Shan mountains in Kazakhstan. Apples do not come true from seed, so every named variety is grafted onto a rootstock, and the rootstock decides how big the tree grows. Most need a pollination partner nearby.

- Grow: plant bare-root trees in winter
- Harvest: late summer to autumn

See also [[Pear]] and [[Japanese Cherry]].
