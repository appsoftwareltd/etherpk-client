*Davallia fejeensis*, in the [[Davalliaceae]] family. Native to the [[Pacific Islands]].

Lacy fronds, and furry rhizomes that creep out over the rim of the pot like small paws. Leave the rhizomes on the surface. Burying them makes them rot.

- Light: medium, indirect
- Water: when the top of the soil is dry

See also [[Blue Star Fern]].
