*Schlumbergera × buckleyi*, in the [[Cactaceae]] family. Native to [[Brazil]].

```map
Rio de Janeiro, Brazil @ -22.41, -42.97
```

A forest cactus that grows on trees in the Brazilian mountains, so it wants more water and less sun than a desert one. It flowers when the nights grow long and cool, which the [[Season Guide]] covers. Moving it while in bud makes the buds drop.

- Light: bright, indirect
- Water: when the top of the soil is dry

See also [[Fishbone Cactus]] and [[Poinsettia]].
