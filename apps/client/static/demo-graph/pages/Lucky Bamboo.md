*Dracaena sanderiana*, in the [[Asparagaceae]] family. Native to [[West Africa]].

Not a bamboo. It is a dracaena whose stems are grown straight, cut, and sometimes trained into spirals. It lives happily in a vase of water for years, as long as the water is changed now and then.

- Light: medium, indirect
- Water: keep the roots covered and change the water every couple of weeks

See also [[Dragon Tree]] and [[Snake Plant]].
