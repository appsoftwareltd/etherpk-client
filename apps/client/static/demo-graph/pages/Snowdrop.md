*Galanthus nivalis*, in the [[Amaryllidaceae]] family. Native to [[Europe]].

The first flower of the year, nodding white bells that push up through frozen ground. Dry bulbs often fail, so snowdrops are best planted "in the green", just after flowering with their leaves on.

- Grow: plant in the green, in late winter
- Flowers: midwinter

See also [[Daffodil]] and [[Christmas Rose]].
