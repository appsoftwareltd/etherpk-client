*Pilea peperomioides*, in the [[Urticaceae]] family. Native to [[China]].

```map
Yunnan, China @ 25, 101.8
Sichuan, China @ 30.6, 103
```

Round, flat leaves on long stalks, like coins on sticks. It spread across Europe in the twentieth century almost entirely by people giving away the babies, long before garden centres stocked it. Turn it now and then, or it leans hard towards the window.

- Light: bright, indirect
- Water: when the top half of the pot is dry

See also [[Baby Rubber Plant]].
