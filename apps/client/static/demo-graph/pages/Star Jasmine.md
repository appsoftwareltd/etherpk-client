*Trachelospermum jasminoides*, in the [[Apocynaceae]] family. Native to [[China]] and [[Japan]].

An evergreen climber with white, star-shaped flowers whose scent carries across a garden in summer. Despite the name it is not a jasmine. It is a relative of the [[Hoya]].

- Grow: sun or part shade, sheltered
- Flowers: midsummer

See also [[Common Jasmine]] and [[Hoya]].
