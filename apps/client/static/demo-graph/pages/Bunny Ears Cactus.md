*Opuntia microdasys*, in the [[Cactaceae]] family. Native to [[Mexico]].

Flat pads in pairs, like rabbit ears, dotted with soft-looking tufts. The tufts are glochids: tiny barbed bristles that come off at a touch and are maddening to remove from skin. Handle it with folded newspaper.

- Light: full sun
- Water: when completely dry

See also [[Prickly Pear]].
