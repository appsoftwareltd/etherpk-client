## How to pick one

1. Be honest about your light. North-facing and gloomy is fine, it just narrows the list.
2. Be honest about your attention span. A fern in the care of a forgetful person is a countdown.
3. Buy one. Not four. One.

The [[Plant Index]] has the rest, a page for each species, grouped into houseplants, herbs, crops and garden plants.
