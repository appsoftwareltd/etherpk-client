*Hoya kerrii*, in the [[Apocynaceae]] family. Native to [[Southeast Asia]].

Sold around Valentine's Day as a single heart-shaped leaf in a small pot. Many of those leaves were cut without a node and will never grow, though they stay alive for years. Buy one with a bit of stem attached if you want a plant.

- Light: bright, indirect
- Water: when the pot is dry

See also [[Hoya]].
