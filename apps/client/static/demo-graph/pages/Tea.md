*Camellia sinensis*, in the [[Theaceae]] family. Native to [[China]].

Every kind of tea, green, black or white, comes from this one shrub. The difference is in how the leaves are processed. It is a [[Camellia]], hardy enough to grow in mild British gardens, and there are tea estates in Cornwall and Scotland.

- Grow: acid soil, shelter, part shade
- Harvest: the top two leaves and a bud

See also [[Camellia]] and [[Coffee]].
