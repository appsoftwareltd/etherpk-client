*Passiflora caerulea*, in the [[Passifloraceae]] family. Native to [[Brazil]] and [[South America]].

An extraordinary flower, which Spanish missionaries in South America read as a picture of the Passion of Christ: the crown of thorns, the nails, the wounds. The orange fruit is edible but dull. Its cousin [[Passion Fruit]] is the one to eat.

- Grow: full sun, sheltered wall
- Flowers: summer to autumn

See also [[Passion Fruit]].
