*Impatiens walleriana*, in the [[Balsaminaceae]] family. Native to [[East Africa]].

The flower for shady bedding and baskets, in every colour, from summer to the first frost. The ripe seed pods explode when touched, which is where the name Impatiens comes from.

- Grow: plant out after the last frost, in shade
- Flowers: summer to autumn

See also [[Fuchsia]].
