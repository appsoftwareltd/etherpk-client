*Acer palmatum*, in the [[Sapindaceae]] family. Native to [[Japan]].

A small tree with delicate, hand-shaped leaves that turn scarlet, orange or gold in autumn. It wants shelter from wind and hot afternoon sun, which scorch the leaves. Many varieties are slow enough for a pot.

- Grow: dappled shade, shelter
- Colour: spring and autumn

See also [[Japanese Cherry]].
