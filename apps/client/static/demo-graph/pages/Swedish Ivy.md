*Plectranthus verticillatus*, in the [[Lamiaceae]] family. Native to [[Southern Africa]].

Neither Swedish nor an ivy. It is a trailing relative of mint from southern Africa, named because it was so popular in Swedish homes. Rub a leaf and it smells faintly of the mint family.

- Light: bright, indirect
- Water: when the top of the soil is dry

See also [[Tradescantia]] and [[Spearmint]].
