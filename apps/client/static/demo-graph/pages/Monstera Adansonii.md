*Monstera adansonii*, in the [[Araceae]] family. Native to [[Central America]] and [[South America]].

```map
Costa Rica @ 9.9, -84.1
Panama @ 8.6, -80.1
Colombia @ 4.6, -74.5
Peruvian Amazon @ -4.2, -75
Amazonas, Brazil @ -3.5, -62.5
```

The smaller cousin of the [[Monstera]]. Its leaves are full of holes from the start, and the holes stay holes rather than growing into splits. It trails from a shelf or climbs a pole, and it grows fast once it settles.

- Light: bright, indirect
- Water: when the top few centimetres are dry

See also [[Monstera]] and [[Mini Monstera]].
