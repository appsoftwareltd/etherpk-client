*Jasminum officinale*, in the [[Oleaceae]] family. Native to the [[Himalaya]] and [[China]].

A twining climber with sweet-scented white flowers through summer, strongest in the evening. It is a relative of the [[Olive]].

- Grow: full sun, sheltered
- Flowers: summer

See also [[Star Jasmine]] and [[Honeysuckle]].
