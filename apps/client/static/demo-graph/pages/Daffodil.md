*Narcissus pseudonarcissus*, in the [[Amaryllidaceae]] family. Native to [[Europe]].

The wild daffodil is small and pale, and every big garden daffodil was bred from it and its relatives. After flowering, leave the leaves for six weeks to feed the [[Bulb]], however untidy they look. Daffodils are a national emblem of Wales.

- Grow: plant bulbs in early autumn
- Flowers: early spring

See also [[Snowdrop]] and [[Tulip]].
