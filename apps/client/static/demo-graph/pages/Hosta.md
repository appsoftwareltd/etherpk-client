*Hosta sieboldiana*, in the [[Asparagaceae]] family. Native to [[Japan]].

Grown for its huge, ribbed, blue-green leaves, which light up shady corners. It is also a slug and snail banquet, and the leaves can be lace by midsummer (see [[Pests]]).

- Grow: shade or part shade, moist soil
- Flowers: summer

See also [[Lily of the Valley]].
