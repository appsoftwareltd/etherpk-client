*Capsicum annuum*, in the [[Solanaceae]] family. Native to [[Mexico]] and [[Central America]].

Sweet peppers and most chillies are the same species, bred for different amounts of capsaicin, the chemical that makes them hot. It is a good crop for a sunny windowsill, and the fruit gets hotter when the plant is kept a little dry.

- Grow: sow indoors early in the year, in warmth
- Harvest: green or fully coloured, through late summer

See also [[Tomato]] and [[Aubergine]].
