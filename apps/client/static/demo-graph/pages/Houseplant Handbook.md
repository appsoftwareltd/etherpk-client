A short guide to [[Watering]], [[Repotting]] and six plants that forgive you, as a PDF. Click the link to read it in a tab of its own, without leaving your notes.

- The book: [Houseplant Handbook](../assets/houseplant-handbook.14b9fb60.pdf)
  - Chapters on light, water and soil come first, then [[Monstera]], [[Snake Plant]], [[Golden Pothos]], [[Spider Plant]], [[Peace Lily]] and [[Aloe Vera]]
  - A link can go straight to one page: [the plant sitter's card](../assets/houseplant-handbook.14b9fb60.pdf#page=14) is the last one, ready to fill in and sign
  - Page 12 is [a plate of a leaf](../assets/houseplant-handbook.14b9fb60.pdf#page=12) with no words on it, so a highlight has to be drawn by hand

Highlight it, draw on it and leave notes on it. The PDF itself never changes: your marks are saved beside it, and **Save a copy** writes a new file with them in.
