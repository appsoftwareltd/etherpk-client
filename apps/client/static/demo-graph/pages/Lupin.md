*Lupinus polyphyllus*, in the [[Fabaceae]] family. Native to [[North America]].

Dense spires of pea flowers in every colour. Like all the pea family, it feeds the soil with nitrogen from its roots. Slugs and aphids love it as much as gardeners do.

- Grow: sow in spring
- Flowers: early summer

See also [[Sweet Pea]] and [[Delphinium]].
