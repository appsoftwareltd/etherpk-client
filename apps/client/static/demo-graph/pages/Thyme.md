*Thymus vulgaris*, in the [[Lamiaceae]] family. Native to the [[Mediterranean]].

```map
Andalusia, Spain @ 37.4, -4.6
Catalonia, Spain @ 41.8, 1.5
Provence, France @ 44, 5.9
```

A low, woody [[Herb]] with tiny leaves and a big flavour. Like most Mediterranean herbs it tastes strongest grown hard: poor soil, full sun, little water. Trim it after flowering or it gets woody and bare in the middle.

- Light: full sun
- Water: rarely

See also [[Rosemary]] and [[Oregano]].
