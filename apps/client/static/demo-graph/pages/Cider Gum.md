*Eucalyptus gunnii*, in the [[Myrtaceae]] family. Native to [[Australia]].

One of the hardiest eucalyptus trees, from the mountains of Tasmania. Young plants have round, silver-blue leaves, the ones florists use. Left alone it becomes a big tree, so many gardeners cut it to the ground every few years to keep the young leaves coming.

- Grow: full sun, sheltered
- Cut: in spring

See also [[Silver Birch]].
