*Pachira aquatica*, in the [[Malvaceae]] family. Native to [[Central America]] and [[South America]].

```map
Guatemala @ 15.5, -90.3
Costa Rica @ 9.9, -84.1
Panama @ 8.6, -80.1
Guyana @ 5, -58.9
Amazonas, Brazil @ -3.5, -62.5
```

Usually sold with several trunks braided together. In the wild it grows in swamps, but in a pot it rots easily, so it is better kept on the dry side.

- Light: bright, indirect
- Water: when the top half of the pot is dry

See also [[Rose of China]].
