*Zingiber officinale*, in the [[Zingiberaceae]] family. Native to [[Southeast Asia]].

The root you buy is a rhizome, and a plump piece with buds will sprout if you plant it shallowly in a pot in spring. It grows tall, reed-like leaves. Dig it up in autumn for fresh ginger.

- Light: bright, indirect, and warm
- Water: keep moist while it grows

See also [[Turmeric]] and [[Lemongrass]].
