*Lilium regale*, in the [[Liliaceae]] family. Native to [[China]].

```map
Min Valley, Sichuan, China @ 31.68, 103.85
```

Tall stems of white trumpets with a scent that fills a garden on summer evenings. Every part of a lily, even the pollen, is poisonous to cats. Unlike the [[Peace Lily]], this is a real lily.

- Grow: plant bulbs in autumn or spring
- Flowers: midsummer

- Ernest Wilson found it in the Min valley, in western Sichuan, in 1903. He went back for bulbs in 1910, and a rock fall on the valley road broke his leg, which left him with a limp for the rest of his life
