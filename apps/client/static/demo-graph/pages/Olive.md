*Olea europaea*, in the [[Oleaceae]] family. Native to the [[Mediterranean]].

```map
Andalusia, Spain @ 37.4, -4.6
Northern Tunisia @ 36.7, 9.2
Crete, Greece @ 35.25, 24.9
Southern Turkey @ 37.1, 33
Cyprus @ 35, 33.1
```

Silvery evergreen leaves and a twisted trunk. Some trees around the Mediterranean are thought to be well over a thousand years old. In a cool garden it makes a handsome tree but rarely ripens fruit.

- Grow: full sun, sharp drainage, shelter from cold wind
- Harvest: autumn, in a long hot summer

See also [[Fig]] and [[Bay Laurel]].
