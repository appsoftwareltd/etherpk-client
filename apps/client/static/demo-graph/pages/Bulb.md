---
title: Bulb
aliases:
  - Bulbs
---
A bulb is an underground store of food that carries a plant through its dormant season, so it can flower fast when conditions are right. Gardeners use the word loosely for corms, tubers and rhizomes too.

Plant spring bulbs in autumn, at about three times their own depth, pointy end up. After flowering, leave the leaves to die back on their own, because they are feeding next year's flower. See [[Tulip]], [[Daffodil]] and [[Crocus]].
