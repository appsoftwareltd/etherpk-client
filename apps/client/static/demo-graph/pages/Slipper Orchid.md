*Paphiopedilum*, in the [[Orchidaceae]] family. Native to [[Southeast Asia]].

```map
Northern Thailand @ 18.8, 98.9
Northern Vietnam @ 21.6, 105
Sabah, Malaysia @ 5.4, 116.9
Sumatra, Indonesia @ -0.6, 101.3
Luzon, Philippines @ 16.5, 121.1
```

Each flower has a pouch like the toe of a slipper. Insects fall into it and can only leave by a route that brushes them past the pollen. Unlike most orchids sold as houseplants, it grows in leaf litter on the forest floor rather than up a tree.

- Light: medium, indirect
- Water: keep evenly moist

See also [[Moth Orchid]].
