*Disocactus anguliger*, in the [[Cactaceae]] family. Native to [[Mexico]].

```map
Nayarit, Mexico @ 21.8, -104.9
Jalisco, Mexico @ 20.6, -103.6
Oaxaca, Mexico @ 17, -96.7
```

Flat, zigzag stems like fish skeletons, trailing from a hanging pot. Like the [[Christmas Cactus]] it is a forest cactus and wants shade and moisture rather than desert treatment. Mature plants flower at night.

- Light: bright, indirect
- Water: when the top of the soil is dry

See also [[Christmas Cactus]] and [[Mistletoe Cactus]].
