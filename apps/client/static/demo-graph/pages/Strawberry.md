*Fragaria × ananassa*, in the [[Rosaceae]] family. A hybrid that arose in France in the eighteenth century between a species from [[North America]] and one from [[South America]].

```map
Virginia, USA @ 37.5, -78.8
Biobío, Chile @ -37.5, -72.4
```

Plants send out runners that root into new plants, so a row becomes a bed in a couple of years. Tuck straw under the fruit to keep it clean and off the soil. Replace plants every three or four years.

- Grow: plant in late summer or spring
- Harvest: early to mid summer

See also [[Raspberry]].
