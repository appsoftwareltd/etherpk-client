title: Lamiaceae

The mint family. Most members have square stems, leaves in opposite pairs, and leaves full of scented oils, which is why the family supplies so much of the [[Herb]] garden: [[Basil]], [[Rosemary]], [[Thyme]], [[Sage]] and the mints themselves.

Every member in this graph links here, so **Backlinks** lists them all.
