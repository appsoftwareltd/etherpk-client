*Epipremnum aureum*, in the [[Araceae]] family. Native to the [[Pacific Islands]].

```map
Moorea, French Polynesia @ -17.54, -149.83
```

The plant that survives when nothing else does. It trails for metres, roots in a glass of water, and puts up with dark corners and missed [[Watering]]. The name devil's ivy comes from how hard it is to kill. In the wild it climbs trees and its leaves grow huge.

- Light: anything but direct sun
- Water: when it starts to droop slightly

See also [[Philodendron]] and [[ZZ Plant]].
