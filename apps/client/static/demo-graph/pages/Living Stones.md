*Lithops*, in the [[Aizoaceae]] family. Native to [[Southern Africa]].

```map
Southern Namibia @ -26.6, 18.1
Namaqualand, South Africa @ -30.2, 17.9
Bushmanland, South Africa @ -29.13, 19.39
```

Pairs of fat leaves that look exactly like pebbles, to hide from grazing animals. Each year a new pair grows from the middle, feeding on the old one. Water it while that happens and it bursts or rots.

- Light: full sun
- Water: only in its growing season, and very little

See also [[Ice Plant]].
