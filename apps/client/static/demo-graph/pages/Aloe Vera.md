*Aloe vera*, in the [[Asphodelaceae]] family. Native to the [[Arabian Peninsula]].

Thick, toothed leaves full of a clear gel that people put on burns. It is a [[Succulent]] that wants more light than most windowsills give, and it rots in wet soil.

- Light: full sun
- Water: when the soil is completely dry

See also [[Zebra Haworthia]] and [[Agave]].
