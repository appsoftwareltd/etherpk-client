*Salvia officinalis*, in the [[Lamiaceae]] family. Native to the [[Mediterranean]].

Soft, grey-green leaves for stuffing and brown butter. It is a shrubby [[Herb]] that gets leggy after a few years and is best replaced from cuttings. Bees love its purple flowers.

- Light: full sun
- Water: rarely, once established

See also [[Rosemary]] and [[Thyme]].
