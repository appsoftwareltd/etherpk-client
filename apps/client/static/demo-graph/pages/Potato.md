*Solanum tuberosum*, in the [[Solanaceae]] family. Native to the [[Andes]].

```map
Cusco, Peru @ -13.5, -71.97
Lake Titicaca, Peru @ -15.84, -70.02
La Paz, Bolivia @ -16.5, -68.15
```

Grown from seed potatoes set out to sprout in a light, cool place in late winter. Pile soil up round the stems as they grow, because tubers that see daylight turn green and poisonous. The leaves and berries are poisonous too, like those of the [[Tomato]], its close relative.

- Grow: plant in spring, earthing up as the shoots appear
- Harvest: earlies when they flower, maincrop when the leaves die back

See also [[Tomato]] and [[Sweet Potato]].
