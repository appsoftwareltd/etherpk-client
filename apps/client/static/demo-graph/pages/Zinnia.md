*Zinnia elegans*, in the [[Asteraceae]] family. Native to [[Mexico]].

```map
Jalisco, Mexico @ 20.6, -103.6
Guanajuato, Mexico @ 21, -101.2
Michoacán, Mexico @ 19.2, -101.9
```

Bright, long-lasting flowers on stiff stems, good for cutting. It needs heat and dislikes root disturbance, so sow it where it is to flower. A zinnia was one of the first flowers grown in space, on the International Space Station.

- Grow: sow in late spring, in warmth
- Flowers: summer

See also [[Cosmos]].
