This is the demo graph. It's about houseplants, because everyone has killed at least one, and it lives in this browser's storage only - so type over anything, tick things, drag blocks about, upload a photo of your own sad fern. Nothing here is precious. **Reset demo** in the bar above puts it all back (on a phone, **Reset the demo** on the Graphs page).

**The basics**

- Press Enter at the end of a bullet to start the next one.
  - Press Tab to nest a bullet under the one above, and Shift+Tab to bring it back out.
- Click [[Reading List]]. There is no page for it yet, so you get a draft. It becomes a page when you type in it.
- [ ] Tick this task. The **Tasks** view lists every task in the graph.
- Type `/` on an empty line for the command menu: tables, dates, images and more.

**What to try**

- Read [The Houseplant Handbook](../assets/houseplant-handbook.14b9fb60.pdf), a PDF, in a tab of its own. Highlight it, draw on it, fill in its form and sign it. [[Houseplant Handbook]] has more links into it.
- Read [The Plant Sitter's Guide](../assets/plant-sitters-guide.aca2cf4e.epub), an EBook, in a tab of its own. Make the text bigger, select a line and highlight it, and press the footnote in the Water chapter.
- Click a link like [[Plant]] and follow the trail. Every `[[double bracket]]` is a concept, and **Backlinks**, in the right sidebar, shows everything that mentions the one you're on. On a phone, the buttons at each end of the top bar open the two sidebars.
- Click [[Yellow Leaves]]. It has no page either, and three pages link to it anyway: they are listed beneath the draft.
- Open the **Tasks** view (right sidebar). The journal entries are full of them, with priorities, due dates and a couple that are waiting on someone else.
- Type `[[` anywhere and pick from the completion.
- Press Alt + Up / Down on a bullet to move the block with its children.
- Look at [[Watering]] for maths, a diagram and a code block rendered in place, and [[Monstera]] for images and a PDF.
- Open the whole **Graph View**: right-click this tab, or on a phone long-press its name in the top bar, and choose **Show in Graph View**. Every page is a dot, every link a line, and each cluster of closely linked pages gets a colour of its own.
- Open any plant, such as [[Coffee]], to see where it grows wild on a map. From the same menu on its tab, choose **Open Map View** for its map alone, or open the Map View of [[Where to Learn About Plants]] for gardens gathered from across the graph.
- [[Where I hide the good fertiliser]] is a protected page. The passphrase is `monstera`. Unlock it from the padlock, have a read, then lock it again.
