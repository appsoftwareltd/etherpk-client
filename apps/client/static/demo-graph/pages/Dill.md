*Anethum graveolens*, in the [[Apiaceae]] family. Native to [[Western Asia]] and the [[Mediterranean]].

Feathery leaves for fish and pickles, and flat yellow flower heads that hoverflies love. Do not grow it next to [[Fennel]]: the two cross-pollinate, and the seed of either then comes up as a muddle of both.

- Light: full sun
- Water: keep moist

See also [[Fennel]] and [[Coriander]].
