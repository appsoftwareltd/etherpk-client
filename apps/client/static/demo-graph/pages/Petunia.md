*Petunia × atkinsiana*, in the [[Solanaceae]] family. Native to [[South America]].

```map
Rio Grande do Sul, Brazil @ -29.7, -53.5
Uruguay @ -32.8, -56
Entre Ríos, Argentina @ -32, -59.2
Buenos Aires, Argentina @ -36.5, -60
```

The hanging basket flower. It is a relative of the [[Tomato]] and the [[Potato]], which is not obvious from the flowers. Pinch off the dead ones and it flowers until the frost.

- Grow: plant out after the last frost
- Flowers: summer to autumn

See also [[Fuchsia]] and [[Tomato]].
