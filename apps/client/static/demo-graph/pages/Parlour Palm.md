*Chamaedorea elegans*, in the [[Arecaceae]] family. Native to [[Mexico]] and [[Central America]].

A small palm that grows in the shade of the forest floor, which is why it copes indoors. Several stems are often sold in one pot to look fuller. It rarely gets taller than a metre inside.

- Light: low to medium
- Water: when the top of the soil is dry

See also [[Kentia Palm]].
