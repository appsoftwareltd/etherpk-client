*Drosera capensis*, in the [[Droseraceae]] family. Native to [[Southern Africa]].

```map
Cape Peninsula, South Africa @ -33.96, 18.4
Western Cape, South Africa @ -33.6, 19.5
```

Strap-shaped leaves covered in red tentacles, each tipped with a drop of glue that glitters like dew. An insect that lands sticks, and the leaf slowly curls round it. It is the easiest [[Carnivorous Plant]] to grow, and it seeds itself into nearby pots.

- Light: bright, with some direct sun
- Water: rainwater, standing in a tray of it

See also [[Round-Leaved Sundew]] and [[Venus Flytrap]].
