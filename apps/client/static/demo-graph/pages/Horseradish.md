*Armoracia rusticana*, in the [[Brassicaceae]] family. Native to [[Europe]] and [[Western Asia]].

A root so pungent that grating it makes the eyes stream. Every scrap of root left in the ground grows a new plant, so give it a corner you will never want back, or a large pot.

- Grow: plant a piece of root in spring
- Harvest: dig roots in autumn

See also [[Cabbage]].
