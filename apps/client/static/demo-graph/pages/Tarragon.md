*Artemisia dracunculus*, in the [[Asteraceae]] family. Native to [[Central Asia]].

The [[Herb]] for béarnaise sauce. French tarragon rarely sets seed, so it is grown from cuttings. Seed sold as tarragon is usually the Russian kind, which is hardier and tastes of very little.

- Light: full sun
- Water: when dry

See also [[Chamomile]].
