*Curcuma longa*, in the [[Zingiberaceae]] family. Native to [[India]] and [[Southeast Asia]].

A relative of [[Ginger]] whose rhizomes are bright orange inside and stain everything they touch. It grows big, tropical leaves and dies back in winter.

- Light: bright, indirect, and warm
- Water: keep moist while it grows

See also [[Ginger]].
