A plant is a living organism that cannot walk away from its problems. This is the main difference between a plant and you. Plants deal with adversity by either thriving heroically or going yellow and crispy - there is no middle ground.

This page answers to *Plants* and *Houseplant* too (see the frontmatter at the top). Links to any of them land here and their backlinks pool together.

## What makes something a plant?

Scientifically, plants are multicellular organisms that turn sunlight into food by photosynthesis. Less scientifically, they're the things you keep buying and then feeling guilty about.

- Needs [[Watering]]
  - but not *too much* [[Watering]] - see [[Yellow Leaves]]
  - and not on a schedule, whatever the label says
- Needs [[Sunlight]]
  - bright and indirect is the phrase you'll see everywhere
  - it means "near a window, not on the sill"
- Lives in [[Soil]]
  - which it will outgrow, hence [[Repotting]]
- Occasionally attacked by [[Pests]]
- Occasionally sulks for no reason at all

## Why have plants?

Research confirms that plants make you happier, improve the air a bit, and make a flat look considerably more put-together than it is. They're also excellent listeners and never criticise your life choices.

## Types of plant

There are roughly 390,000 known species. You'll probably end up with a [[Monstera]], a [[Succulent]], a [[Peace Lily]] and a [[Spider Plant]], because those are the ones every garden centre and supermarket sells. For something more structured, see [[Types of [[Plant]]]].

## Feeding

[[Plants]] need nutrients like you do, except their diet is [[[[Plant]] Foods]] rather than toast and anxiety. See [[Sourcing [[[[Plant]] Foods]]]] for where to get the stuff.

## Seasons

A [[Houseplant]] responds to the seasons and may slow down, drop leaves or look frankly alarming in winter. This is usually fine. Consult the [[Season Guide]] before binning anything in January.
