*Petroselinum crispum*, in the [[Apiaceae]] family. Native to the [[Mediterranean]].

Curly or flat-leaved, and slow to start: the seed can take weeks to germinate. It is a biennial, so it grows leaves in its first year and runs to flower in its second. Sow a fresh batch every spring.

- Light: sun or part shade
- Water: keep moist

See also [[Coriander]] and [[Carrot]].
