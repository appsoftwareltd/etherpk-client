*Phormium tenax*, in the [[Asphodelaceae]] family. Native to [[New Zealand]].

Huge fans of stiff, strap-shaped leaves in green, bronze or striped. The Māori wove the leaf fibre into cloth, rope and baskets. It is not related to the flax that linen comes from.

- Grow: full sun
- Water: in dry spells

See also [[Cabbage Palm]].
