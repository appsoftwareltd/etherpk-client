*Cordyline australis*, in the [[Asparagaceae]] family. Native to [[New Zealand]].

Tufts of sword-shaped leaves on tall trunks, common on English seafronts. It is not a palm. Early European settlers in New Zealand ate the young leaves, which is where the name cabbage tree comes from.

- Grow: full sun, mild coastal gardens
- Water: in dry spells

See also [[Chusan Palm]] and [[New Zealand Flax]].
