*Fatsia japonica*, in the [[Araliaceae]] family. Native to [[Japan]].

Big, glossy, hand-shaped leaves on an evergreen shrub. It lives indoors or out, in shade, and gives a tropical look to a dark corner of a cold garden.

- Light: part shade or shade
- Water: keep moist

See also [[Umbrella Plant]] and [[English Ivy]].
