*Verbena bonariensis*, in the [[Verbenaceae]] family. Native to [[South America]].

Tall, wiry, almost leafless stems topped with small purple flower heads that seem to float. Butterflies cover it. It seeds itself into gravel and cracks in paving.

- Grow: full sun
- Flowers: midsummer to autumn

See also [[Buddleja]].
