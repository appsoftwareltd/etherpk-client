*Haworthiopsis attenuata*, in the [[Asphodelaceae]] family. Native to [[Southern Africa]].

```map
Eastern Cape, South Africa @ -32.3, 26.4
```

A small rosette of dark leaves striped with raised white bumps. It is happy in less light than most succulents, which makes it a good choice for a desk.

- Light: bright, indirect
- Water: when the soil is completely dry

See also [[Aloe Vera]].
