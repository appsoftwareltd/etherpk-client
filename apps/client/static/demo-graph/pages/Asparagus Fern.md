*Asparagus setaceus*, in the [[Asparagaceae]] family. Native to [[Southern Africa]].

```map
Eastern Cape, South Africa @ -32.3, 26.4
KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa @ -28.7, 30.9
Mpumalanga, South Africa @ -25.6, 30.4
Limpopo, South Africa @ -23.9, 29.5
Zimbabwe @ -19, 29.8
```

Feathery and fern-like, and not a fern at all. It is an asparagus, a relative of the one you eat, and some kinds carry small hooked spines. It is a lot tougher than a real [[Fern]].

- Light: bright, indirect
- Water: when the top of the soil is dry

See also [[Asparagus]] and [[Boston Fern]].
