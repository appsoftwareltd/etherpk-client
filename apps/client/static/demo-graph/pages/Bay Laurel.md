*Laurus nobilis*, in the [[Lauraceae]] family. Native to the [[Mediterranean]].

```map
Provence, France @ 44, 5.9
Tuscany, Italy @ 43.4, 11.1
Dalmatia, Croatia @ 43.7, 16.6
Peloponnese, Greece @ 37.5, 22.3
Aegean Turkey @ 38.6, 28
```

An evergreen tree whose leaves flavour stocks and stews. It takes clipping well and is often grown as a lollipop in a pot by the door. The laurel wreaths of ancient Greece were made from it.

- Light: sun or part shade
- Water: when the top of the soil is dry

See also [[Rosemary]].
