*Gladiolus × hortulanus*, in the [[Iridaceae]] family. Native to [[Southern Africa]].

Stiff spikes of big flowers, named from the Latin for a small sword, after the shape of the leaves. Plant corms in batches a few weeks apart for flowers all summer.

- Grow: plant corms in spring
- Flowers: summer

See also [[Freesia]].
