*Cymbidium*, in the [[Orchidaceae]] family. Native to the [[Himalaya]] and [[China]].

```map
Nepal @ 28.2, 84
Sikkim, India @ 27.5, 88.5
Bhutan @ 27.4, 90.4
Yunnan, China @ 25, 101.8
Guizhou, China @ 26.8, 106.8
```

Long, grassy leaves and tall spikes of waxy flowers in winter. It is the orchid that likes it cool: it spends summer outdoors, and the cold autumn nights are what make it flower.

- Light: bright, outdoors in summer
- Water: keep moist in summer, a little drier in winter

See also [[Dendrobium]].
