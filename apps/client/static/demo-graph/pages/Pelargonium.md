*Pelargonium × hortorum*, in the [[Geraniaceae]] family. Native to [[Southern Africa]].

```map
Western Cape, South Africa @ -33.6, 19.5
Eastern Cape, South Africa @ -32.3, 26.4
```

The windowbox "geranium". It is a different genus from the true geraniums, and unlike them it is killed by frost, so bring it in before winter. Take cuttings in late summer and it lives for ever.

- Grow: in pots, in sun
- Flowers: spring to autumn

See also [[Hardy Geranium]].
