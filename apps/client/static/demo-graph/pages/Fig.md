*Ficus carica*, in the [[Moraceae]] family. Native to [[Western Asia]] and the [[Mediterranean]].

```map
Aegean Turkey @ 38.6, 28
Southern Turkey @ 37.1, 33
Western Syria @ 35, 36.4
Georgia @ 42.2, 43.6
Northern Iran @ 36.5, 51.5
```

Big, lobed leaves and fruit that ripen in a hot summer. Given free roots it grows huge and fruits little, so plant it in a pot or a pit lined with paving slabs. The fruit is really a hollow stem lined with tiny flowers.

- Grow: plant against a sunny wall, roots restricted
- Harvest: late summer, when the fruit softens and droops

See also [[Fiddle Leaf Fig]] and [[Olive]].
