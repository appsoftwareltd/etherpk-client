*Tagetes patula*, in the [[Asteraceae]] family. Native to [[Mexico]] and [[Central America]].

```map
Jalisco, Mexico @ 20.6, -103.6
Michoacán, Mexico @ 19.2, -101.9
Hidalgo, Mexico @ 20.5, -98.9
Puebla, Mexico @ 19, -97.9
Guatemala @ 15.5, -90.3
```

Not French. It came to Britain from Mexico by way of France. It is grown beside [[Tomato]] plants in the belief that its smell keeps whitefly away. The evidence is mixed, but the orange is cheerful either way.

- Grow: sow indoors in spring, plant out after frost
- Flowers: all summer

See also [[Pot Marigold]] and [[Tomato]].
