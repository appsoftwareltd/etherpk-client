*Dicksonia antarctica*, in the [[Dicksoniaceae]] family. Native to [[Australia]].

A [[Fern]] with a trunk, topped with a crown of fronds two metres long. The trunk is a mass of roots, so water the trunk itself, from the top, not just the ground around it. It is hardy in milder gardens if the crown is protected in winter.

- Light: dappled shade, outdoors
- Water: pour water into the crown and down the trunk in dry weather

See also [[Royal Fern]].
