*Phaseolus coccineus*, in the [[Fabaceae]] family. Native to [[Mexico]] and [[Central America]].

The allotment staple, climbing a wigwam of canes. It came to Britain in the seventeenth century as a flower for its scarlet blooms, and only later did anyone think to eat the pods. Pick often, because a plant left with old pods stops flowering.

- Grow: sow outdoors in late spring, by a tall support
- Harvest: through summer, before the beans inside swell

See also [[French Bean]] and [[Pea]].
