*Pyrus communis*, in the [[Rosaceae]] family. Native to [[Europe]] and [[Western Asia]].

```map
Thuringia, Germany @ 50.9, 11
Bulgaria @ 42.7, 25.3
Anatolia, Turkey @ 39, 34
Georgia @ 42.2, 43.6
Northern Iraq @ 36.2, 44
```

Unlike [[Apple]], most pears are picked before they are ripe and finished indoors, or they go grainy and brown at the core. They flower earlier than apples, so a late frost can take the crop.

- Grow: plant bare-root trees in winter
- Harvest: late summer to autumn, while still firm

See also [[Apple]].
