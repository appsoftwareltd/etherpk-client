*Dieffenbachia seguine*, in the [[Araceae]] family. Native to the [[Caribbean]] and [[South America]].

```map
Puerto Rico @ 18.25, -66.45
Dominica @ 15.42, -61.35
Trinidad @ 10.45, -61.25
Venezuela @ 7.5, -66
Colombia @ 4.6, -74.5
```

Big leaves splashed with cream. The sap is full of needle-like calcium oxalate crystals, and chewing a leaf makes the mouth and throat swell so badly that speaking is difficult, hence the name. Keep it away from pets and small children.

- Light: bright, indirect
- Water: when the top few centimetres are dry

See also [[Chinese Evergreen]].
