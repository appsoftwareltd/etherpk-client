*Crassula ovata*, in the [[Crassulaceae]] family. Native to [[Southern Africa]].

```map
Eastern Cape, South Africa @ -32.3, 26.4
KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa @ -28.7, 30.9
Mozambique @ -18.7, 35.5
```

A [[Succulent]] that grows into a small tree with a thick trunk and fat, glossy leaves. It lives for decades, and old plants are often handed down. A little stress from sun and dryness turns the leaf edges red.

- Light: as bright as you have
- Water: when the soil is completely dry

See also [[Elephant Bush]] and [[Echeveria]].
