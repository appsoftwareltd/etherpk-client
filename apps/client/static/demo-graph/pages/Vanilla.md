*Vanilla planifolia*, in the [[Orchidaceae]] family. Native to [[Mexico]].

A climbing orchid, and the source of real vanilla. Each flower opens for a single day. Outside Mexico there is no insect to pollinate it, so every vanilla pod is the result of someone pollinating a flower by hand, using a method worked out by a twelve-year-old enslaved boy, Edmond Albius, on the island of Réunion in 1841.

- Light: bright, indirect
- Water: keep evenly moist, with humid air

See also [[Moth Orchid]].
