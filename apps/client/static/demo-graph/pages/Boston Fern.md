*Nephrolepis exaltata*, in the [[Nephrolepidaceae]] family. Native to the [[Caribbean]] and [[Central America]].

```map
Cuba @ 22.1, -79.6
Jamaica @ 18.15, -77.3
Puerto Rico @ 18.25, -66.45
Honduras @ 14.8, -86.6
Costa Rica @ 9.9, -84.1
```

The classic [[Fern]], with long arching fronds that look wonderful on a pedestal and shed tiny leaflets all over the floor when the air is dry. It wants constant moisture without sitting wet, which is a hard balance. Overwatering it is how fungus gnats arrive (see [[Pests]]).

- Light: bright, indirect
- Water: keep evenly moist, and stand it on a tray of wet pebbles

See also [[Maidenhair Fern]] and [[Bird's Nest Fern]].
