*Ocimum basilicum*, in the [[Lamiaceae]] family. Native to [[India]] and [[Southeast Asia]].

The tender [[Herb]] that every kitchen windowsill tries and mostly kills. It hates cold, wet roots and draughts. Pinch out the tips above a pair of leaves and it branches, and pick off any flowers before they open. It belongs with [[Tomato]] on the plate and in the greenhouse.

- Light: full sun, and warmth
- Water: in the morning, at the base, when the top of the soil is dry

See also [[Tomato]] and [[Spearmint]].
