*Utricularia vulgaris*, in the [[Lentibulariaceae]] family. Native to [[Europe]].

```map
Norfolk, England @ 52.65, 1.1
Netherlands @ 52.2, 5.6
Masuria, Poland @ 53.8, 21.3
Finland @ 62.5, 25
```

A rootless water plant whose underwater stems carry hundreds of tiny bladders. When a water flea brushes a trigger hair, the bladder sucks it in, one of the fastest movements in the plant world. Above the water it holds up small yellow flowers.

- Light: full sun, in a pond
- Water: it lives in it

See also [[Butterwort]].
