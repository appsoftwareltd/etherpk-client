---
title: Carnivorous Plant
aliases:
  - Carnivorous Plants
---
Carnivorous plants grow in bogs and on poor soils with almost no nitrogen, so they catch insects to make up for it. They still make their food from sunlight like any other plant.

The rules are strict. Rainwater or distilled water only, never tap water. No fertiliser, ever. And no scraps of meat, whatever you have heard: catching their own flies is enough.

Different plants found different traps: snap traps on the [[Venus Flytrap]], glue on the [[Cape Sundew]] and the [[Butterwort]], pitfalls on the [[Trumpet Pitcher]] and the [[Tropical Pitcher Plant]], and suction on the [[Bladderwort]].
