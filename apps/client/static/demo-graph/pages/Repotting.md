---
title: Repotting
---
Roots need space too, same as the rest of us. Signs it's time:

- Roots out of the drainage holes
- Water runs straight through without soaking in
- The plant dries out days faster than it used to
- It's been two years and you've never done it

## How

1. Go one pot size up. Not three. A small plant in a huge pot sits in wet [[Soil]] and rots.
2. Fresh compost, and a handful of perlite if you have it.
3. Tease the roots loose a little. Don't be precious about it.
4. Water it in, then leave it alone for a week to sulk.

Spring is the time - see the [[Season Guide]]. A [[Monstera]] will forgive you for doing it in autumn. A fern will not.
