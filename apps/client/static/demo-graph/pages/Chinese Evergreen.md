*Aglaonema commutatum*, in the [[Araceae]] family. Native to [[Southeast Asia]].

Patterned leaves in silver, green and sometimes pink, on a plant that genuinely tolerates low light. It grows slowly and stays compact. It dislikes cold more than almost anything else, so keep it away from winter windows.

- Light: low to medium
- Water: when the top half of the pot is dry

See also [[Dumb Cane]] and [[ZZ Plant]].
