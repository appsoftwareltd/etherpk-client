title: Fern

---
Ferns have no flowers or seeds. They spread by spores, released from small brown patches under their fronds, and they were around long before the dinosaurs.

Most ferns grown indoors come from damp forest floors and want humid air, steady moisture and no direct sun, which makes a bright bathroom ideal. Not everything called a fern is one: the [[Asparagus Fern]] is a flowering plant.
