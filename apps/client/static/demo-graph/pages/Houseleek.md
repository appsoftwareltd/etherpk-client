*Sempervivum tectorum*, in the [[Crassulaceae]] family. Native to [[Europe]].

A hardy [[Succulent]] that lives outdoors all year, even through frost. Its name tectorum means "of the roofs": it was planted on roofs in the belief that it kept lightning away. Each rosette flowers once and dies, after making plenty of offsets.

- Light: full sun, outdoors
- Water: rarely. Rain usually does it

See also [[Echeveria]] and [[Aeonium]].
