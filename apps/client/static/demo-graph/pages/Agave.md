*Agave americana*, in the [[Asparagaceae]] family. Native to [[Mexico]].

A huge rosette of grey, spine-tipped leaves. After ten to thirty years it sends up a flower stalk several metres tall, flowers once, and dies. Mind the spines.

- Light: full sun
- Water: rarely

See also [[Aloe Vera]] and [[Yucca]].
