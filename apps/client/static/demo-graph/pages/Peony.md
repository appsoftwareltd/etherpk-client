*Paeonia lactiflora*, in the [[Paeoniaceae]] family. Native to [[China]].

```map
Heilongjiang, China @ 47, 128
Jilin, China @ 43.5, 126.5
Inner Mongolia, China @ 42, 112
Hebei, China @ 38.6, 115.5
Shanxi, China @ 37.6, 112.3
```

Huge, scented, blousy flowers for a couple of weeks in early summer. It hates being moved and can take years to forgive you, but left alone a peony can outlive the gardener. Ants on the buds are after the sugar and do no harm.

- Grow: full sun, planted shallow
- Flowers: early summer

See also [[Rose]].
