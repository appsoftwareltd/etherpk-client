*Aphelandra squarrosa*, in the [[Acanthaceae]] family. Native to [[Brazil]].

```map
Espírito Santo, Brazil @ -19.6, -40.6
Rio de Janeiro, Brazil @ -22.41, -42.97
São Paulo, Brazil @ -23, -47.5
```

Dark leaves striped with white veins, topped with a yellow flower spike. It is often treated as a short-lived gift plant, but with warmth and steady moisture it lives for years.

- Light: bright, indirect
- Water: keep evenly moist, never dry

See also [[Nerve Plant]].
