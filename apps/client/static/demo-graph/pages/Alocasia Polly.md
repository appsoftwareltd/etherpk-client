*Alocasia × amazonica*, in the [[Araceae]] family. A nursery hybrid of two species from [[Southeast Asia]].

```map
Mindanao, Philippines @ 7.7, 125
Peninsular Malaysia @ 3.8, 102.5
Sarawak, Malaysia @ 2.5, 113
```

Dark arrow-shaped leaves with bright white veins. It is named after the Florida nursery that bred it, not the river, and it is not from Africa either. It goes dormant if it gets cold, dropping every leaf, and comes back from the bulb-like corm in spring.

- Light: bright, indirect
- Water: keep slightly moist in summer, much drier in winter

See also [[Arrowhead Plant]].
