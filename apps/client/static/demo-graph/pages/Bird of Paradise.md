*Strelitzia reginae*, in the [[Strelitziaceae]] family. Native to [[Southern Africa]].

Big paddle-shaped leaves and, on a mature plant in good light, orange and blue flowers shaped like a bird's head. It is named after Queen Charlotte, who was born into the house of Mecklenburg-Strelitz. The leaves split in the wind, which is normal.

- Light: as bright as possible, some direct sun
- Water: when the top few centimetres are dry

See also [[Banana]].
