*Pinguicula moranensis*, in the [[Lentibulariaceae]] family. Native to [[Mexico]].

```map
Jalisco, Mexico @ 20.6, -103.6
Michoacán, Mexico @ 19.2, -101.9
Hidalgo, Mexico @ 20.5, -98.9
Puebla, Mexico @ 19, -97.9
Oaxaca, Mexico @ 17, -96.7
```

A flat rosette of greasy-looking leaves that are covered in tiny glue glands. It catches fungus gnats very efficiently, which makes it a useful [[Carnivorous Plant]] to keep among your other [[Plants]] (see [[Pests]]).

- Light: bright, indirect
- Water: rainwater, keep moist in summer, drier in winter

See also [[Cape Sundew]] and [[Bladderwort]].
