*Prunus serrulata*, in the [[Rosaceae]] family. Native to [[China]] and Korea, and bred into hundreds of garden cherries in [[Japan]].

```map
Zhejiang, China @ 29.2, 120.1
Anhui, China @ 31.8, 117.2
Jiangxi, China @ 27.6, 115.7
Hubei, China @ 31, 112.3
Liaoning, China @ 41.3, 122.6
```

Clouds of pink or white blossom for a couple of weeks in spring. In Japan the blossom is celebrated with picnics under the trees, called hanami. It is grown for flowers, not fruit.

- Grow: full sun
- Flowers: spring

- Shinjuku Gyoen, in Tokyo, has about a thousand cherry trees of over sixty kinds, which flower at different times through the spring [[Where to Learn About Plants]]
  ```map
  Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden @ 35.68507, 139.70955
  ```
