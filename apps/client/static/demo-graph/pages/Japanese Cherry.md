*Prunus serrulata*, in the [[Rosaceae]] family. Native to [[Japan]] and [[China]].

Clouds of pink or white blossom for a couple of weeks in spring. In Japan the blossom is celebrated with picnics under the trees, called hanami. It is grown for flowers, not fruit.

- Grow: full sun
- Flowers: spring

See also [[Japanese Maple]] and [[Apple]].
