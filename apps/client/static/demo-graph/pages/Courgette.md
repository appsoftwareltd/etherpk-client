*Cucurbita pepo*, in the [[Cucurbitaceae]] family. Native to [[Mexico]].

```map
Tamaulipas, Mexico @ 24, -98.8
Puebla, Mexico @ 19, -97.9
Oaxaca, Mexico @ 17, -96.7
```

One plant feeds a family, and two feed the street. Pick the fruits small and often, because a courgette left for a week becomes a marrow. The big yellow flowers are edible too.

- Grow: sow indoors in late spring, plant out after the last frost
- Harvest: every couple of days in summer

See also [[Pumpkin]] and [[Cucumber]].
