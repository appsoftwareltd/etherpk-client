*Rosa*, in the [[Rosaceae]] family. Garden roses are hybrids of species from [[China]], [[Western Asia]] and [[Europe]].

```map
Sichuan, China @ 30.6, 103
Hubei, China @ 31, 112.3
Northern Iran @ 36.5, 51.5
Anatolia, Turkey @ 39, 34
Central France @ 46.8, 1.7
```

Shrubs, climbers and ramblers in thousands of varieties. Most want sun, rich soil and a good feed in spring (see [[[[Plant]] Foods]]). Aphids, black spot and mildew are the usual troubles (see [[Pests]]). Deadheading keeps repeat-flowering kinds going all summer.

- Grow: full sun, rich soil
- Flowers: early summer, and again in many varieties

See also [[Peony]] and [[Apple]].
