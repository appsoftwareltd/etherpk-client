*Nepeta cataria*, in the [[Lamiaceae]] family. Native to [[Europe]] and [[Western Asia]].

```map
Central France @ 46.8, 1.7
Bulgaria @ 42.7, 25.3
Ukraine @ 49, 31.4
Anatolia, Turkey @ 39, 34
Northern Iran @ 36.5, 51.5
```

A grey-leaved mint relative that sends most cats, though not all, into a rolling, drooling trance. Plant it from seed rather than as a young plant, because a bruised seedling attracts every cat in the street and gets flattened.

- Light: full sun
- Water: when dry

See also [[Spearmint]].
