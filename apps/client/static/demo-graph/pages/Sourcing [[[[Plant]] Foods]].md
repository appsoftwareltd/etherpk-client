So you've decided to feed your [[Plants]]. Excellent. This page is scoped two levels deep - "Sourcing" within [[[[Plant]] Foods]], which is itself within [[Plant]] - and the link above resolves all the way down.

## Garden centres

The classic. Walk in, get overwhelmed, buy three things you didn't need, leave. They stock a bewildering range of feeds, decent [[Soil]], and staff who actually know things (use them).

**Verdict:** great. Leave your card in the car.

## Supermarkets

A small bottle near the pots and watering cans. It works perfectly well. Nobody needs to know.

**Verdict:** fine. Convenient. Slightly uninspiring.

## Online

The internet will sell you professional quantities for a fraction of the price. Useful if you have a lot of [[Plants]] or have fully committed to the bit.

**Verdict:** excellent value. You'll have more [[[[Plant]] Foods]] than you can use in a lifetime.

## Steal from a friend

A handful of someone else's slow-release granules is practically a tradition.

**Verdict:** 10/10, sustainable, builds community.
