*Microsorum musifolium*, in the [[Polypodiaceae]] family. Native to [[Southeast Asia]].

```map
Peninsular Malaysia @ 3.8, 102.5
Sumatra, Indonesia @ -0.6, 101.3
Sarawak, Malaysia @ 2.5, 113
Luzon, Philippines @ 16.5, 121.1
```

Long, undivided fronds patterned with a net of dark veins, like crocodile skin. It is a showpiece [[Fern]] for a humid bathroom.

- Light: medium, indirect
- Water: keep evenly moist

See also [[Blue Star Fern]].
