The palms. Most need far more light than a room offers, so the few sold as houseplants, such as the [[Kentia Palm]] and the [[Parlour Palm]], are the shade-tolerant ones. Several plants called palms are not palms at all: see the [[Sago Palm]], the [[Ponytail Palm]] and the [[Cabbage Palm]].
