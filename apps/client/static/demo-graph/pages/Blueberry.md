*Vaccinium corymbosum*, in the [[Ericaceae]] family. Native to [[North America]].

A shrub that needs acid soil, so most gardeners grow it in a pot of ericaceous compost and water it with rainwater, because tap water slowly raises the pH (see [[Soil]]). Two varieties side by side give a bigger crop. The autumn colour is a bonus.

- Grow: plant in ericaceous compost
- Harvest: midsummer onwards

See also [[Heather]] and [[Raspberry]].
