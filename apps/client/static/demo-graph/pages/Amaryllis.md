*Hippeastrum*, in the [[Amaryllidaceae]] family. Native to [[South America]].

The giant [[Bulb]] sold in winter that grows a stem and four huge trumpet flowers in a few weeks on a windowsill. It is really a Hippeastrum. The true Amaryllis is a different plant from southern Africa.

- Grow: pot with the top third of the bulb showing
- Flowers: winter, indoors

See also [[Agapanthus]].
