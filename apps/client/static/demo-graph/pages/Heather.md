*Calluna vulgaris*, in the [[Ericaceae]] family. Native to [[Europe]].

```map
Scottish Highlands @ 57.3, -4.5
North York Moors, England @ 54.37, -0.9
Southern Norway @ 60.5, 8.5
Lüneburg Heath, Germany @ 53.17, 9.95
Netherlands @ 52.2, 5.6
```

The purple of upland moors in late summer. It is a low, woody shrub that needs acid soil and full sun, and the bees that work it make heather honey.

- Grow: full sun, acid soil
- Flowers: late summer to autumn

See also [[Blueberry]] and [[Azalea]].
