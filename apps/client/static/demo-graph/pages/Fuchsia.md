*Fuchsia magellanica*, in the [[Onagraceae]] family. Native to [[South America]].

```map
Los Lagos, Chile @ -41, -72.8
Chiloé, Chile @ -42.6, -73.9
Aysén, Chile @ -45.57, -72.07
Neuquén, Argentina @ -38.9, -70.1
Tierra del Fuego, Argentina @ -54.3, -67.7
```

Dangling red and purple flowers like dancers. It comes from the far south of South America, and in mild, wet places such as the west of Ireland it has escaped into hedges.

- Grow: sun or part shade
- Flowers: summer to autumn

See also [[Petunia]].
