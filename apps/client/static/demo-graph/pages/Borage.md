*Borago officinalis*, in the [[Boraginaceae]] family. Native to the [[Mediterranean]].

Hairy leaves that taste faintly of cucumber, and bright blue star-shaped flowers that float in summer drinks. Bees visit it constantly. Sow it once and it comes back from seed every year after.

- Light: full sun
- Water: when dry

See also [[Forget-Me-Not]] and [[Cucumber]].
