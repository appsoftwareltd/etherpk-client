*Coffea arabica*, in the [[Rubiaceae]] family. Native to [[East Africa]].

Glossy dark leaves on a small shrub from the highlands of Ethiopia. It makes a handsome houseplant, and in a bright room an older plant may produce scented white flowers and red berries, though not enough for a cup.

- Grow: as a houseplant, in bright, indirect light
- Harvest: a few berries, if you are lucky

See also [[Tea]].
