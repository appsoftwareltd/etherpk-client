*Tillandsia usneoides*, in the [[Bromeliaceae]] family. Native to [[North America]] and [[South America]].

```map
Louisiana, USA @ 30.9, -92.2
Georgia, USA @ 31.5, -82.6
Venezuela @ 7.5, -66
Bolivia @ -17, -64.7
Tucumán, Argentina @ -26.8, -65.2
```

Grey, hair-like strands that drape from trees across the American south and further. It is neither Spanish nor a moss: it is a rootless relative of the [[Pineapple]] that lives on rain and the dust in it.

- Light: bright, indirect
- Water: mist often, or dunk weekly

See also [[Air Plant]].
