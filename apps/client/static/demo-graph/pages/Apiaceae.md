The carrot family, with flowers held in flat umbrellas called umbels. It gives the kitchen [[Carrot]], [[Parsley]], [[Coriander]] and [[Dill]]. It also contains hemlock, one of the most poisonous plants in Europe, which looks a lot like its edible cousins. Never forage from this family unless you are sure.
