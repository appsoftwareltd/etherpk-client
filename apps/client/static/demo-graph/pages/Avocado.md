*Persea americana*, in the [[Lauraceae]] family. Native to [[Mexico]] and [[Central America]].

Most avocado plants in Britain start as a stone suspended over a glass of water on cocktail sticks. It roots, sprouts, and grows into an attractive houseplant. Fruit indoors is unlikely: a tree from seed takes years to flower and needs warmth and light it will not get.

- Grow: from a stone, broad end down, just touching water
- Harvest: not indoors

See also [[Bay Laurel]].
