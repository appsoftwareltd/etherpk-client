*Delphinium elatum*, in the [[Ranunculaceae]] family. Native to [[Europe]] and [[Central Asia]].

```map
French Alps @ 45, 6.4
Austrian Alps @ 47.2, 13
Carpathians, Romania @ 45.6, 25
Altai, Russia @ 50.6, 86.2
Kazakhstan @ 48, 68
```

Towering spires of blue flowers, the centrepiece of a cottage garden border. It needs staking before it needs it, and protection from slugs in spring.

- Grow: full sun, rich soil, stakes
- Flowers: early summer

See also [[Lupin]] and [[Clematis]].
