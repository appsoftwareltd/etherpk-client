*Syngonium podophyllum*, in the [[Araceae]] family. Native to [[Central America]] and [[South America]].

```map
Guatemala @ 15.5, -90.3
Costa Rica @ 9.9, -84.1
Panama @ 8.6, -80.1
Colombia @ 4.6, -74.5
Bolivia @ -17, -64.7
```

Young leaves are arrow-shaped. As the plant matures and starts to climb, the leaves split into three, five, or more lobes, so an old plant looks like a different species. Pinch the climbing stems to keep it bushy.

- Light: medium to bright, indirect
- Water: when the top of the soil is dry

See also [[Alocasia Polly]] and [[Philodendron]].
