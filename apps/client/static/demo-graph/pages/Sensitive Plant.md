*Mimosa pudica*, in the [[Fabaceae]] family. Native to [[Central America]] and [[South America]].

Touch a leaf and its leaflets fold shut, one pair after another, and the whole stalk droops. A few minutes later it opens again. It is easy to grow from seed, and children never tire of it.

- Light: bright, with some direct sun
- Water: keep evenly moist

See also [[Prayer Plant]].
