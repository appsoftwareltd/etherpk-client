*Rhododendron simsii*, in the [[Ericaceae]] family. Native to [[China]].

The pot azalea sold in winter, covered in flowers. It needs ericaceous compost and rainwater, and it dies quickly if it dries out even once.

- Grow: cool, bright room in winter, shade outdoors in summer
- Flowers: winter, indoors

See also [[Heather]] and [[Camellia]].
