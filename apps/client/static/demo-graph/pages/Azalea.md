*Rhododendron simsii*, in the [[Ericaceae]] family. Native to [[China]].

```map
Hunan, China @ 27.6, 111.7
Jiangxi, China @ 27.6, 115.7
Zhejiang, China @ 29.2, 120.1
Fujian, China @ 26.1, 118
Guangdong, China @ 23.4, 113.5
```

The pot azalea sold in winter, covered in flowers. It needs ericaceous compost and rainwater, and it dies quickly if it dries out even once.

- Grow: cool, bright room in winter, shade outdoors in summer
- Flowers: winter, indoors

- The Royal Botanic Garden Edinburgh has one of the largest collections of wild rhododendrons, many grown from seed collected in China [[Where to Learn About Plants]]
  ```map
  Royal Botanic Garden Edinburgh @ 55.96529, -3.20886
  ```
