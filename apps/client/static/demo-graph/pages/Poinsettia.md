*Euphorbia pulcherrima*, in the [[Euphorbiaceae]] family. Native to [[Mexico]].

The red "petals" are leaves called bracts. They colour up only after weeks of long nights, which is why getting one to turn red a second year means shutting it in a dark cupboard every evening from autumn on. Most are thrown out after the holidays. The sap irritates skin.

- Light: bright, indirect
- Water: when the top of the soil is dry, and never let it sit in water

See also [[Christmas Cactus]] and [[Croton]].
