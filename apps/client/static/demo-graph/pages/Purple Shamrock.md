title: Purple Shamrock
aliases:
  - Oxalis triangularis
  - False shamrock
---
*Oxalis triangularis*, in the [[Oxalidaceae]] family. Native to [[Brazil]].

Purple leaves in threes, like butterfly wings, that fold down at night like a [[Prayer Plant]]. It grows from small bulbs, and if it collapses it is resting, not dead. Stop watering, wait a few weeks, and it comes back.

- Light: bright, indirect
- Water: when the top of the soil is dry

See also [[Prayer Plant]].
