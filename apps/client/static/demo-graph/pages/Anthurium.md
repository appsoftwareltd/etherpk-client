*Anthurium andraeanum*, in the [[Araceae]] family. Native to [[South America]].

```map
Nariño, Colombia @ 1.3, -77.9
Esmeraldas, Ecuador @ 0.7, -79.3
```

The glossy red "flower" is a spathe, as on a [[Peace Lily]], and it lasts for weeks. Grown well, an anthurium flowers on and off all year. It likes warmth and humid air and sulks in a cold draught.

- Light: bright, indirect
- Water: when the top of the soil is dry

See also [[Peace Lily]].
