*Melissa officinalis*, in the [[Lamiaceae]] family. Native to the [[Mediterranean]] and [[Western Asia]].

Leaves that smell strongly of lemon when you brush past. It is a mint relative and seeds itself everywhere, so cut it back before it flowers.

- Light: sun or part shade
- Water: when dry

See also [[Spearmint]].
