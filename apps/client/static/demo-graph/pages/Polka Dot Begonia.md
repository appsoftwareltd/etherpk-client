*Begonia maculata*, in the [[Begoniaceae]] family. Native to [[Brazil]].

Olive leaves covered in silver spots, with red undersides, on bamboo-like canes. It grows tall and needs a stake, and pinching the tips keeps it bushy.

- Light: bright, indirect
- Water: when the top of the soil is dry

See also [[Rex Begonia]].
