*Beta vulgaris*, in the [[Amaranthaceae]] family. Native to [[Europe]] and the [[Mediterranean]].

Chard, sugar beet and beetroot are all one species, descended from the sea beet of European coasts. Each wrinkled "seed" is a cluster of several, so seedlings come up in clumps and need thinning. The leaves are good to eat too.

- Grow: sow outdoors from spring
- Harvest: anything from golf ball to cricket ball size

See also [[Carrot]].
