*Howea forsteriana*, in the [[Arecaceae]] family. Native to [[Australia]].

```map
Lord Howe Island, Australia @ -31.55, 159.08
```

The palm that made palm courts. It comes from one small island east of Australia, Lord Howe Island, and became the Victorian houseplant of choice because it tolerates the shade of a real room. It grows slowly, so mature plants are expensive.

- Light: medium, indirect
- Water: when the top few centimetres are dry

See also [[Parlour Palm]] and [[Cast Iron Plant]].
