*Dracaena trifasciata*, in the [[Asparagaceae]] family. Native to [[West Africa]].

Stiff, upright leaves banded in dark and pale green. It tolerates low light, dry air and weeks of neglect, which makes it the plant people buy after killing everything else. It was called Sansevieria until botanists moved it into Dracaena.

- Light: anything from a dim corner to a sunny window
- Water: when the soil is completely dry. In winter, once a month is plenty

See also [[ZZ Plant]] and [[Lucky Bamboo]].
