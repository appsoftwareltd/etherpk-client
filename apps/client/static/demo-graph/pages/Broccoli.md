*Brassica oleracea Italica Group*, in the [[Brassicaceae]] family. Native to the [[Mediterranean]].

```map
Campania, Italy @ 40.9, 14.8
Calabria, Italy @ 39, 16.4
Sicily, Italy @ 37.6, 14.1
```

The part we eat is a head of unopened flower buds. Cut the main head and smaller side shoots follow for weeks. Bred in Italy, as the name suggests.

- Grow: sow in spring
- Harvest: before the buds open

See also [[Cabbage]] and [[Kale]].
