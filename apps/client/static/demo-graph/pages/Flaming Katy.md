*Kalanchoe blossfeldiana*, in the [[Crassulaceae]] family. Native to [[Madagascar]].

```map
Tsaratanana Massif, Madagascar @ -14, 48.95
```

A succulent sold in flower in every supermarket, in red, orange, pink or yellow. Like the [[Poinsettia]], it flowers after weeks of long nights, so getting it to bloom again means a dark spot every evening in autumn.

- Light: bright, with some direct sun
- Water: when the soil is dry

See also [[Mother of Thousands]].
