*Freesia refracta*, in the [[Iridaceae]] family. Native to [[Southern Africa]].

Arching stems of scented trumpet flowers, a favourite with florists. It grows from a small corm, and in cool climates it is best grown in pots under glass.

- Grow: plant corms in spring, in pots
- Flowers: summer

See also [[Gladiolus]].
