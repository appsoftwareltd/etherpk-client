*Peperomia obtusifolia*, in the [[Piperaceae]] family. Native to the [[Caribbean]] and [[Central America]].

```map
Jamaica @ 18.15, -77.3
Puerto Rico @ 18.25, -66.45
Guatemala @ 15.5, -90.3
Costa Rica @ 9.9, -84.1
Panama @ 8.6, -80.1
```

Fleshy, rounded leaves on a compact plant. It is not a rubber plant, though it looks like a small one. Peperomias are related to black pepper and store water in their leaves, so they forgive missed watering.

- Light: medium to bright, indirect
- Water: when the pot is nearly dry

See also [[Watermelon Peperomia]] and [[Rubber Plant]].
