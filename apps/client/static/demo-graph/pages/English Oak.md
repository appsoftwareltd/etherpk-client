*Quercus robur*, in the [[Fagaceae]] family. Native to [[Europe]].

```map
Southern England @ 51.15, -1.2
Central France @ 46.8, 1.7
Thuringia, Germany @ 50.9, 11
Białowieża Forest, Poland @ 52.7, 23.85
Southern Sweden @ 57, 14.8
```

The great native tree of Britain. A single oak supports more kinds of insect, bird, fungus and lichen than any other native tree, and it can live for a thousand years.

- Grow: plant an acorn, then wait
- Acorns: autumn

- The Major Oak, in Sherwood Forest, is thought to be between 800 and 1,100 years old, and props hold up its branches
  ```map
  Major Oak, Sherwood Forest @ 53.20466, -1.07247
  ```

See also [[Silver Birch]] and [[Holly]].
