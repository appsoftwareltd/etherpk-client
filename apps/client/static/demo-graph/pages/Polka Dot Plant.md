*Hypoestes phyllostachya*, in the [[Acanthaceae]] family. Native to [[Madagascar]].

Leaves spattered with pink, red or white. It is grown for those leaves, so pinch off the small flowers, which make the plant leggy and short-lived.

- Light: bright, indirect
- Water: keep evenly moist

See also [[Nerve Plant]].
