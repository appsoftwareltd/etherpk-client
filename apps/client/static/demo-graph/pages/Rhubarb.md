*Rheum × hybridum*, in the [[Polygonaceae]] family. Native to [[China]].

```map
Heilongjiang, China @ 47, 128
Jilin, China @ 43.5, 126.5
Inner Mongolia, China @ 42, 112
Hebei, China @ 38.6, 115.5
```

Grown for its stalks, used as a fruit. The leaves are poisonous with oxalic acid, so never eat them. Covering a crown with a bucket in late winter forces pale, tender early stalks. Leave a new plant unpicked for its first year.

- Grow: plant crowns in autumn or early spring, then leave them for decades
- Harvest: pull, do not cut, from spring to early summer

See also [[Sorrel]].
