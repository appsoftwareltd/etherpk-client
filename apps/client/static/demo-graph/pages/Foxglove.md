*Digitalis purpurea*, in the [[Plantaginaceae]] family. Native to [[Europe]].

Tall spires of spotted purple bells that bumblebees climb right inside. It is a biennial: leaves in the first year, flowers in the second. Every part is poisonous, and it is also the source of digoxin, a drug for heart failure.

- Grow: sow in summer for flowers the next year
- Flowers: early summer

See also [[Snapdragon]].
