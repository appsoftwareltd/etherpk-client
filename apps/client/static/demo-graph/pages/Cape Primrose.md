*Streptocarpus × hybridus*, in the [[Gesneriaceae]] family. Native to [[Southern Africa]].

```map
Eastern Cape, South Africa @ -32.3, 26.4
KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa @ -28.7, 30.9
```

Long, crinkled leaves and trumpet flowers on wiry stems. It is a relative of the [[African Violet]] and easier to please. Cut off the flower stems at the base when they finish and more follow.

- Light: bright, indirect
- Water: when the top of the soil is dry

See also [[African Violet]].
