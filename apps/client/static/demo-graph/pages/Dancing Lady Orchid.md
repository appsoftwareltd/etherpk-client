*Oncidium*, in the [[Orchidaceae]] family. Native to [[Central America]] and [[South America]].

Sprays of small yellow and brown flowers, each shaped like a dancer in a wide skirt. Some kinds smell of chocolate.

- Light: bright, indirect
- Water: when the bark is nearly dry

See also [[Moth Orchid]].
