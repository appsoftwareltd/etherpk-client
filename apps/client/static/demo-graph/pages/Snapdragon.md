*Antirrhinum majus*, in the [[Plantaginaceae]] family. Native to the [[Mediterranean]].

Squeeze the sides of a flower and it opens like a mouth. Only a bee heavy enough can push the lips apart to get in. Good for cutting.

- Grow: sow indoors early in spring
- Flowers: summer to autumn

See also [[Foxglove]].
