*Buddleja davidii*, in the [[Scrophulariaceae]] family. Native to [[China]].

```map
Sichuan, China @ 30.6, 103
Yunnan, China @ 25, 101.8
Guizhou, China @ 26.8, 106.8
Hubei, China @ 31, 112.3
Shaanxi, China @ 35.2, 108.9
```

Long purple spikes of honey-scented flowers that draw butterflies in crowds. It seeds itself into walls, railway sidings and old chimneys. Cut it back hard every spring.

- Grow: full sun, any soil
- Flowers: summer

See also [[Verbena]].
