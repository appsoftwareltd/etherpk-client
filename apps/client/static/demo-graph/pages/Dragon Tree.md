*Dracaena marginata*, in the [[Asparagaceae]] family. Native to [[Madagascar]].

Tufts of thin, red-edged leaves on bare, bendy stems. It sheds its lower leaves as it grows, which is normal and not a sign of trouble. Cut a stem and it branches below the cut.

- Light: bright, indirect
- Water: when the top half of the pot is dry

See also [[Lucky Bamboo]] and [[Yucca]].
