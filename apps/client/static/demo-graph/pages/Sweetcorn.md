*Zea mays*, in the [[Poaceae]] family. Native to [[Mexico]].

A grass, and a tall one. It is pollinated by the wind, so plant it in a block rather than a row, or the cobs fill out in patches. The sugar in a cob starts turning to starch the moment it is picked, so have the water boiling first.

- Grow: sow indoors in late spring, plant out in a block
- Harvest: when the tassels on the cob turn brown

See also [[Pumpkin]] and [[French Bean]].
