*Wisteria sinensis*, in the [[Fabaceae]] family. Native to [[China]].

A climber with long hanging chains of scented lilac flowers in late spring. It is vigorous enough to pull down a weak trellis. Prune twice a year, in summer and winter, to make it flower rather than just grow.

- Grow: full sun, very strong support
- Flowers: late spring

See also [[Sweet Pea]].
