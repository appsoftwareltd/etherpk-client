*Dionaea muscipula*, in the [[Droseraceae]] family. Native to [[North America]].

```map
North Carolina, USA @ 35, -77.8
South Carolina, USA @ 33.7, -80.5
```

The best-known [[Carnivorous Plant]], from a small area of bog in the Carolinas. Each trap shuts only when its trigger hairs are touched twice within about twenty seconds, so a raindrop does not set it off. A trap only closes a handful of times before it dies, so do not set them off for fun.

- Light: full sun
- Water: rainwater only, standing in a tray of it

See also [[Cape Sundew]] and [[Trumpet Pitcher]].
