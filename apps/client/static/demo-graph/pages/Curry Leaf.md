*Murraya koenigii*, in the [[Rutaceae]] family. Native to [[India]].

```map
Kerala, India @ 10.5, 76.3
Karnataka, India @ 14.5, 75.7
Tamil Nadu, India @ 11.1, 78.6
Assam, India @ 26.2, 92.9
Uttarakhand, India @ 30.1, 79
```

A small tree whose leaves are fried in oil to start many South Indian dishes. It has nothing to do with curry powder. It is a houseplant in cooler countries, and it drops its leaves if it gets cold.

- Light: bright, with some direct sun
- Water: when the top of the soil is dry

See also [[Lemon]].
