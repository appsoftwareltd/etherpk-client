*Cosmos bipinnatus*, in the [[Asteraceae]] family. Native to [[Mexico]].

Feathery leaves and simple pink, white or crimson flowers on tall stems. The more you cut it, the more it flowers. It does best in poor soil, and rich soil gives you leaves instead.

- Grow: sow in spring
- Flowers: midsummer to autumn

See also [[Dahlia]] and [[Zinnia]].
