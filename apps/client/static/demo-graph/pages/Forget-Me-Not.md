*Myosotis sylvatica*, in the [[Boraginaceae]] family. Native to [[Europe]].

```map
Southern England @ 51.15, -1.2
Thuringia, Germany @ 50.9, 11
Swiss Alps @ 46.6, 8.2
Austrian Alps @ 47.2, 13
Poland @ 52, 19.4
```

A haze of tiny sky-blue flowers in spring. It seeds itself everywhere and is easy to pull out where it is not wanted. A cousin of [[Borage]].

- Grow: sow once and it is yours for life
- Flowers: spring

See also [[Borage]] and [[Bluebell]].
