*Origanum vulgare*, in the [[Lamiaceae]] family. Native to [[Europe]] and the [[Mediterranean]].

```map
Southern England @ 51.15, -1.2
Thuringia, Germany @ 50.9, 11
Tuscany, Italy @ 43.4, 11.1
Peloponnese, Greece @ 37.5, 22.3
Aegean Turkey @ 38.6, 28
```

A spreading [[Herb]] whose flavour is much stronger dried than fresh. The pink summer flowers are covered in bees and butterflies.

- Light: full sun
- Water: when dry

See also [[Thyme]] and [[Basil]].
