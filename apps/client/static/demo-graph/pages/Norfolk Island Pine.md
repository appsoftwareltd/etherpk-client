*Araucaria heterophylla*, in the [[Araucariaceae]] family. Native to the [[Pacific Islands]].

Tiers of soft, needled branches. It is not a pine, and it is often sold at Christmas as a living tree that can stay indoors all year. It needs good light or the lower branches drop.

- Light: bright, with some direct sun
- Water: when the top of the soil is dry
