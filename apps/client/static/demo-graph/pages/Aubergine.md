*Solanum melongena*, in the [[Solanaceae]] family. Native to [[India]].

A [[Tomato]] relative with glossy purple fruit and soft, spiny-stemmed leaves. In a cool summer it wants a greenhouse. The name eggplant comes from the old white varieties, which really do look like eggs.

- Grow: sow indoors early, in warmth
- Harvest: while the skin is still glossy

See also [[Chilli Pepper]].
