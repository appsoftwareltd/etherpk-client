*Araucaria araucana*, in the [[Araucariaceae]] family. Native to [[South America]].

```map
Biobío, Chile @ -37.5, -72.4
Araucanía, Chile @ -38.7, -72.4
Neuquén, Argentina @ -38.9, -70.1
```

A tall, prehistoric-looking conifer with branches covered in sharp, scale-like leaves. The name comes from a Victorian quip that climbing it would puzzle a monkey. It is a relative of the [[Norfolk Island Pine]].

- Grow: full sun, plenty of room
- Water: rarely, once established

See also [[Norfolk Island Pine]].
