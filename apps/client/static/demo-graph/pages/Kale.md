*Brassica oleracea Acephala Group*, in the [[Brassicaceae]] family. Native to [[Europe]].

A [[Cabbage]] that never forms a head. It is the toughest of the family, standing through winter, and frost makes the leaves sweeter. Pick the lower leaves and it keeps growing upwards.

- Grow: sow in spring
- Harvest: autumn through winter

See also [[Cabbage]] and [[Broccoli]].
