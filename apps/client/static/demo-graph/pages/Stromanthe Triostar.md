*Stromanthe thalia*, in the [[Marantaceae]] family. Native to [[Brazil]].

Leaves variegated in green, cream and pink, with deep pink undersides that show when the leaves lift at night. It wants the same fussing as the calatheas.

- Light: bright, indirect
- Water: keep evenly moist

See also [[Calathea Orbifolia]].
