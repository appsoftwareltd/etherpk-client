*Portulacaria afra*, in the [[Didiereaceae]] family. Native to [[Southern Africa]].

Small round leaves on red stems, often shaped into bonsai. In its home it is eaten by elephants, which is how it got the name. It looks like a [[Jade Plant]] and is not related.

- Light: full sun
- Water: when the soil is dry

See also [[Jade Plant]].
