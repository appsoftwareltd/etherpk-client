*Foeniculum vulgare*, in the [[Apiaceae]] family. Native to the [[Mediterranean]].

```map
Portugal @ 39.6, -8
Andalusia, Spain @ 37.4, -4.6
Sicily, Italy @ 37.6, 14.1
Peloponnese, Greece @ 37.5, 22.3
Cyprus @ 35, 33.1
```

A tall, feathery [[Herb]] that tastes of aniseed, in green or bronze. The swollen fennel bulb from the greengrocer is a variety of the same plant. It seeds itself freely.

- Light: full sun
- Water: when dry

See also [[Dill]].
