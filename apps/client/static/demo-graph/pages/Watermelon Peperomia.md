*Peperomia argyreia*, in the [[Piperaceae]] family. Native to [[South America]].

Round leaves striped silver and green like a watermelon rind, on red stalks. A single leaf with a bit of stalk roots in water or damp compost.

- Light: medium, indirect
- Water: when the top half of the pot is dry

See also [[Baby Rubber Plant]].
