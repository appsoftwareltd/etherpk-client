*Yucca gigantea*, in the [[Asparagaceae]] family. Native to [[Mexico]] and [[Central America]].

Sold as thick, leafless canes with tufts of sword-shaped leaves on top. It wants far more light than most rooms give it, and leans towards the window to say so. Overwatering is the usual way to lose one.

- Light: direct sun is welcome
- Water: when the top half of the pot is dry

See also [[Ponytail Palm]] and [[Agave]].
