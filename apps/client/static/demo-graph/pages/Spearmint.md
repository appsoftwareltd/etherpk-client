*Mentha spicata*, in the [[Lamiaceae]] family. Native to [[Europe]] and [[Western Asia]].

The everyday mint for sauce, tea and new potatoes. It spreads by runners under the soil and will take over a bed in a season, so grow it in a pot, even one sunk into the ground. It is almost impossible to kill.

- Light: sun or part shade
- Water: keep moist

See also [[Lemon Balm]] and [[Catnip]].
