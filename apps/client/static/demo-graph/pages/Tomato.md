*Solanum lycopersicum*, in the [[Solanaceae]] family. Wild tomatoes come from the [[Andes]]. Farmers in [[Mexico]] developed the large-fruited tomato.

```map
Ecuador @ -1.5, -78.5
Peru @ -9.5, -76.5
Veracruz, Mexico @ 19.5, -96.9
Puebla, Mexico @ 19, -97.9
```

The crop everyone with a sunny wall tries first. Cordon types are grown up a cane with the side shoots pinched out, bush types are left to sprawl. Once the first fruits set, feed weekly with a high-potash feed from [[[[Plant]] Foods]]. Irregular [[Watering]] makes the fruit split. Grow [[Basil]] and [[French Marigold]] alongside, one for the kitchen and one for the whitefly.

- Grow: start indoors in spring, plant out after the last frost
- Harvest: through late summer, as the fruit colours

See also [[Potato]], [[Chilli Pepper]] and [[Basil]].
