*Cephalocereus senilis*, in the [[Cactaceae]] family. Native to [[Mexico]].

A column cactus covered in long white hair that shades it from the desert sun. The hair hides sharp spines. Do not water from above, or the hair turns grey and matted.

- Light: full sun
- Water: when completely dry

See also [[Saguaro]].
