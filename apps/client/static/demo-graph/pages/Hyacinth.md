*Hyacinthus orientalis*, in the [[Asparagaceae]] family. Native to [[Western Asia]].

```map
Southern Turkey @ 37.1, 33
Western Syria @ 35, 36.4
Lebanon @ 33.9, 35.8
Northern Israel @ 32.9, 35.4
```

A dense spike of scented flowers. Bulbs sold as "prepared" have been chilled to flower indoors in midwinter. Wear gloves to handle the bulbs, which can irritate skin.

- Grow: plant bulbs in autumn, or force them in pots
- Flowers: spring, or winter indoors

See also [[Bluebell]].
