*Allium schoenoprasum*, in the [[Amaryllidaceae]] family. Native to [[Europe]] and [[Central Asia]].

```map
Swiss Alps @ 46.6, 8.2
Southern Norway @ 60.5, 8.5
Altai, Russia @ 50.6, 86.2
Almaty, Kazakhstan @ 43.2, 76.9
Kyrgyzstan @ 41.5, 74.6
```

Grassy, hollow leaves with a mild onion taste, and purple pompom flowers that are edible too. It is one of the easiest herbs, and it comes back every spring. Dig up and split a clump every few years.

- Light: sun or part shade
- Water: keep moist

See also [[Onion]] and [[Wild Garlic]].
