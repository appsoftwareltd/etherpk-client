*Delosperma cooperi*, in the [[Aizoaceae]] family. Native to [[Southern Africa]].

A low, spreading [[Succulent]] for the garden, covered in shiny magenta flowers all summer. It is one of several plants sold as ice plant, and it is hardy if its roots stay dry in winter.

- Light: full sun
- Water: rarely, once established

See also [[Living Stones]] and [[Houseleek]].
