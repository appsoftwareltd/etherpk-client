*Codiaeum variegatum*, in the [[Euphorbiaceae]] family. Native to [[Southeast Asia]] and the [[Pacific Islands]].

Leaves in yellow, orange, red and green all at once. The colours need bright light, and fade to green without it. It drops leaves when moved, then recovers.

- Light: bright, with some direct sun
- Water: when the top of the soil is dry

See also [[Poinsettia]].
