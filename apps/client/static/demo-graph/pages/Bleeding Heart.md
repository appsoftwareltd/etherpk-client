*Lamprocapnos spectabilis*, in the [[Papaveraceae]] family. Native to [[China]].

```map
Heilongjiang, China @ 47, 128
Jilin, China @ 43.5, 126.5
Liaoning, China @ 41.3, 122.6
```

Arching stems hung with pink heart-shaped flowers, each with a white drop at the tip. It flowers in spring, then often dies back by midsummer, so mark where it is.

- Grow: part shade, moist soil
- Flowers: spring

See also [[Common Poppy]].
