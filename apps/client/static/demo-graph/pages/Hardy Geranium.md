*Geranium sanguineum*, in the [[Geraniaceae]] family. Native to [[Europe]].

```map
The Burren, Ireland @ 53.05, -9.1
Yorkshire Dales, England @ 54.2, -2.2
Thuringia, Germany @ 50.9, 11
Hungary @ 47.2, 19.4
```

The real geranium, as opposed to the [[Pelargonium]]. The seed pods look like a crane's beak, which gives the family its common name. It is tough, spreads into weed-smothering clumps, and flowers for months.

- Grow: sun or part shade, any soil
- Flowers: early summer to autumn

See also [[Pelargonium]].
