A herb, in the kitchen sense, is a plant grown for its leaves or seeds as flavouring. They split roughly in two. Woody Mediterranean ones, such as [[Rosemary]], [[Thyme]] and [[Sage]], want full sun, poor soil and very little water. Soft leafy ones, such as [[Basil]], [[Parsley]] and [[Coriander]], want richer soil and regular watering. A sunny windowsill suits both, for a while.
