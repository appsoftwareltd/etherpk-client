*Echeveria elegans*, in the [[Crassulaceae]] family. Native to [[Mexico]].

A tight rosette of pale blue-green leaves that looks like a stone flower. Echeverias go leggy and lose their shape in poor light, stretching towards the window.

- Light: full sun
- Water: when the soil is completely dry. Keep water out of the rosette

See also [[Houseleek]] and [[Jade Plant]].
