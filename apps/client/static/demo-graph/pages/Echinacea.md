*Echinacea purpurea*, in the [[Asteraceae]] family. Native to [[North America]].


Pink-purple petals around a spiky orange cone. It is a prairie plant that bees and butterflies crowd onto, and if the seed heads are left standing in winter, goldfinches eat them.

- Grow: full sun, well-drained soil
- Flowers: midsummer to autumn

See also [[Sunflower]].
