*Aeonium arboreum*, in the [[Crassulaceae]] family. Native to the [[Canary Islands]].

Rosettes like flat flowers on bare stems, in green or almost black. Unlike most succulents it grows in the cool, wet months and rests in the heat of summer, when the rosettes close up.

- Light: full sun, but shelter from the hottest days
- Water: more in winter, very little in summer

See also [[Houseleek]].
