*Ilex aquifolium*, in the [[Aquifoliaceae]] family. Native to [[Europe]].

```map
Southern England @ 51.15, -1.2
Ireland @ 53.2, -8
Central France @ 46.8, 1.7
Asturias, Spain @ 43.3, -5.9
Tuscany, Italy @ 43.4, 11.1
```

Glossy, spiny evergreen leaves and red winter berries. Holly plants are male or female, and only females carry berries, so you need a male nearby. The leaves high on an old tree are often smooth, out of reach of grazing animals.

- Grow: sun or shade
- Berries: winter

See also [[Box]] and [[English Oak]].
