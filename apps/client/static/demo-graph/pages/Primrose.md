*Primula vulgaris*, in the [[Primulaceae]] family. Native to [[Europe]].

Pale yellow flowers in early spring, in banks and woodland edges. The name comes from the Latin prima rosa, "first rose", though it is no relative of the [[Rose]].

- Grow: part shade, moist soil
- Flowers: early spring

See also [[Cyclamen]].
