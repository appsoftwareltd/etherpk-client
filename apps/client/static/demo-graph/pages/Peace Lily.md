*Spathiphyllum wallisii*, in the [[Araceae]] family. Native to [[Central America]] and [[South America]].

Not a lily. The white "flower" is a hooded leaf called a spathe, wrapped around the real flowers on a spike. It is famous for collapsing dramatically when thirsty and standing back up within an hour of [[Watering]], which makes it the easiest plant to read.

- Light: medium, indirect. Copes with shade but flowers less
- Water: when it starts to droop, or just before

See also [[Anthurium]] and [[Nerve Plant]].
