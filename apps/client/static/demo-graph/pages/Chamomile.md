*Matricaria chamomilla*, in the [[Asteraceae]] family. Native to [[Europe]] and [[Western Asia]].

Small daisy flowers that smell of apple, dried for tea. It is an annual that seeds itself freely and is happy in poor soil.

- Light: full sun
- Water: when dry

See also [[Common Daisy]] and [[Tarragon]].
