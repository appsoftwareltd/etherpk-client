*Zamioculcas zamiifolia*, in the [[Araceae]] family. Native to [[East Africa]].

```map
Coastal Kenya @ -4.2, 39.4
Zanzibar, Tanzania @ -6.1, 39.3
Usambara Mountains, Tanzania @ -4.8, 38.4
Malawi @ -13.3, 34
Mozambique @ -18.7, 35.5
```

Glossy leaves that look plastic, on stems that grow from fat underground rhizomes. The rhizomes store water, so it can go a month without a drink. The usual way to kill one is kindness: too much water rots those rhizomes.

- Light: low to bright, indirect
- Water: once the pot is completely dry

See also [[Snake Plant]] and [[Golden Pothos]].
