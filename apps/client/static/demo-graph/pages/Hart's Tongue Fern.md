*Asplenium scolopendrium*, in the [[Aspleniaceae]] family. Native to [[Europe]].

A native British [[Fern]] with long, undivided leaves like a deer's tongue. It grows in damp shade, on old walls and the banks of lanes, and keeps its leaves through winter.

- Light: shade, outdoors
- Water: keep the soil moist

See also [[Bird's Nest Fern]] and [[Royal Fern]].
