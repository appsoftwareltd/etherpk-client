*Asparagus officinalis*, in the [[Asparagaceae]] family. Native to [[Europe]] and [[Western Asia]].

```map
Central France @ 46.8, 1.7
Hungary @ 47.2, 19.4
Ukraine @ 49, 31.4
Anatolia, Turkey @ 39, 34
Georgia @ 42.2, 43.6
```

A crop that rewards patience: plant crowns, wait two years before cutting any spears, and the bed then produces for twenty years. Stop cutting by early summer and let the ferny foliage grow to feed the crowns. It is a cousin of the [[Asparagus Fern]].

- Grow: plant crowns in spring, in a bed of their own
- Harvest: spears in late spring, from the third year

See also [[Asparagus Fern]].
