*Beaucarnea recurvata*, in the [[Asparagaceae]] family. Native to [[Mexico]].

Not a palm. The swollen base stores water, which makes it as easy to keep as a [[Succulent]], and the fountain of thin leaves on top gives it the name. In the wild it grows into a tree many metres tall over a very long time.

- Light: as bright as you have
- Water: when the soil is completely dry

See also [[Yucca]] and [[Sago Palm]].
