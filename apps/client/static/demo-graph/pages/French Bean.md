*Phaseolus vulgaris*, in the [[Fabaceae]] family. Native to [[Central America]] and [[South America]].

```map
Guatemala @ 15.5, -90.3
Costa Rica @ 9.9, -84.1
Colombia @ 4.6, -74.5
Peru @ -9.5, -76.5
Salta, Argentina @ -24.8, -65.4
```

Dwarf and climbing kinds, eaten as green pods or dried as haricot, kidney and black beans, which are all this one species. It was domesticated twice, separately, in Mexico and in the Andes.

- Grow: sow outdoors once the soil is warm
- Harvest: pods while slim, or leave to dry on the plant

See also [[Runner Bean]], [[Pumpkin]] and [[Sweetcorn]].
