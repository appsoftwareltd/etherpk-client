The orchids, one of the two largest families of flowering plants, with over twenty-five thousand species. Their seeds are as fine as dust and carry no food store, so in the wild each one needs a particular fungus to germinate. Most of those sold as houseplants grow on trees in the wild and want bark rather than soil.
