*Cymbopogon citratus*, in the [[Poaceae]] family. Native to [[Southeast Asia]].

A tall grass whose leaf bases flavour Thai and Vietnamese cooking. Stalks from the supermarket often root in a glass of water within a couple of weeks, and then grow on in a pot. It needs to come indoors before the first frost.

- Light: full sun, and warmth
- Water: keep moist in summer

See also [[Ginger]].
