*Lathyrus odoratus*, in the [[Fabaceae]] family. Native to the [[Mediterranean]].

```map
Campania, Italy @ 40.9, 14.8
Calabria, Italy @ 39, 16.4
Sicily, Italy @ 37.6, 14.1
```

A climbing annual grown for its scent. The more you pick, the more it flowers: let it set seed and it stops. Despite the name, the seeds are poisonous, unlike those of the garden [[Pea]].

- Grow: sow in autumn or early spring, in deep pots
- Flowers: early to late summer

See also [[Pea]] and [[Lupin]].
