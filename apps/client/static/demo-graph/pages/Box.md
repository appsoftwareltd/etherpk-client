*Buxus sempervirens*, in the [[Buxaceae]] family. Native to [[Europe]].

The small-leaved evergreen of hedges, knot gardens and topiary. Box blight and the caterpillars of the box moth have killed a lot of it in recent years (see [[Pests]]).

- Grow: sun or shade
- Clip: early summer

See also [[Holly]].
