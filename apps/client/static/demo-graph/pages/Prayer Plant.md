*Maranta leuconeura*, in the [[Marantaceae]] family. Native to [[Brazil]].

```map
Bahia, Brazil @ -12.5, -41.7
Espírito Santo, Brazil @ -19.6, -40.6
Rio de Janeiro, Brazil @ -22.41, -42.97
```

Patterned leaves that lie flat by day and fold upwards at night, like hands in prayer. A joint at the base of each leaf does the moving. Watch it in the evening and you can see it happen.

- Light: medium, indirect
- Water: keep slightly moist, with rain or filtered water if you can

See also [[Rattlesnake Plant]] and [[Purple Shamrock]].
