*Hyacinthoides non-scripta*, in the [[Asparagaceae]] family. Native to [[Europe]].

```map
Southern England @ 51.15, -1.2
Wales @ 52.3, -3.7
Scottish Highlands @ 57.3, -4.5
Ireland @ 53.2, -8
Brittany, France @ 48.2, -2.9
```

The blue haze of British woods in spring. About half the world's bluebells grow in Britain, and they are protected by law. The Spanish bluebell, more upright with paler flowers, hybridises with it in gardens.

- Grow: dappled shade, under trees
- Flowers: spring

See also [[Wild Garlic]] and [[Hyacinth]].
