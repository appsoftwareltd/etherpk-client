*Drosera rotundifolia*, in the [[Droseraceae]] family. Native to [[Europe]] and [[North America]].

A tiny native [[Carnivorous Plant]] of British bogs, a rosette of round sticky leaves no bigger than a coin. Charles Darwin spent years experimenting on it and wrote a book about it.

- Light: full sun, outdoors in a bog
- Water: always wet, with rainwater

See also [[Cape Sundew]].
