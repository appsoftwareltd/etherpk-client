*Helianthus annuus*, in the [[Asteraceae]] family. Native to [[North America]].

Each flower head is hundreds of tiny flowers packed in a spiral. Young heads follow the sun across the sky, but once they open they settle facing east, which warms them in the morning and brings more bees. Easy to grow, and the seed feeds birds through winter.

- Grow: sow outdoors in late spring
- Flowers: late summer

See also [[Common Daisy]] and [[Cosmos]].
