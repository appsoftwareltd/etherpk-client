*Trachycarpus fortunei*, in the [[Arecaceae]] family. Native to [[China]].

```map
Hubei, China @ 31, 112.3
Sichuan, China @ 30.6, 103
Guizhou, China @ 26.8, 106.8
Yunnan, China @ 25, 101.8
```

A palm that survives British winters, with fan-shaped leaves on a trunk wrapped in hairy fibre. It is one of the few real palms that can live outdoors in a cold climate.

- Grow: sun or part shade, sheltered from strong wind
- Water: in dry spells

See also [[Cabbage Palm]] and [[Kentia Palm]].
