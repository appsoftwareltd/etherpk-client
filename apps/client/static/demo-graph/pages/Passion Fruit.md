*Passiflora edulis*, in the [[Passifloraceae]] family. Native to [[Brazil]].

```map
São Paulo, Brazil @ -23, -47.5
Paraná, Brazil @ -24.6, -51.6
Santa Catarina, Brazil @ -27.3, -50.2
Rio Grande do Sul, Brazil @ -29.7, -53.5
```

A vigorous climber with the same extraordinary flowers as the [[Passion Flower]], followed by wrinkled purple fruit. It needs a warm greenhouse or conservatory to fruit in a cool climate.

- Grow: in a big pot, under glass
- Harvest: when the fruit wrinkles and drops

See also [[Passion Flower]].
