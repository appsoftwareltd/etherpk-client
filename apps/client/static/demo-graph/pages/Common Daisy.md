*Bellis perennis*, in the [[Asteraceae]] family. Native to [[Europe]].

The daisy of lawns and daisy chains. The name comes from "day's eye", because the flowers close at night and open in the morning. Its family, the [[Asteraceae]], is one of the largest of all.

- Grow: it grows itself
- Flowers: almost all year

See also [[Sunflower]] and [[Chamomile]].
