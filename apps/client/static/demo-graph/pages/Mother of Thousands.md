*Kalanchoe daigremontiana*, in the [[Crassulaceae]] family. Native to [[Madagascar]].

```map
Toliara, Madagascar @ -23, 44.2
```

Tiny plantlets form all along the leaf edges, drop off, and root in any pot nearby. One plant becomes dozens, then hundreds. It is poisonous to pets.

- Light: bright
- Water: when the soil is dry

See also [[Flaming Katy]].
