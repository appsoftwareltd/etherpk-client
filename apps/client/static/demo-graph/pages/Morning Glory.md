*Ipomoea purpurea*, in the [[Convolvulaceae]] family. Native to [[Mexico]] and [[Central America]].

```map
Jalisco, Mexico @ 20.6, -103.6
Michoacán, Mexico @ 19.2, -101.9
Puebla, Mexico @ 19, -97.9
Oaxaca, Mexico @ 17, -96.7
Guatemala @ 15.5, -90.3
```

A fast annual climber with trumpet flowers that open in the morning and fade by afternoon. The seeds are poisonous. It is a sister of the [[Sweet Potato]].

- Grow: sow in late spring after soaking the seed
- Flowers: summer

See also [[Sweet Potato]].
