*Ficus elastica*, in the [[Moraceae]] family. Native to [[India]] and [[Southeast Asia]].

```map
Assam, India @ 26.2, 92.9
Meghalaya, India @ 25.5, 91.4
Myanmar @ 21, 96
Sumatra, Indonesia @ -0.6, 101.3
Java, Indonesia @ -7.3, 110
```

Thick, glossy leaves, often deep burgundy. Its milky sap was once tapped for rubber, before the rubber tree took over. Wipe the leaves now and then, because dust blocks the light and they show every speck.

- Light: bright, indirect
- Water: when the top half of the pot is dry

See also [[Fiddle Leaf Fig]].
