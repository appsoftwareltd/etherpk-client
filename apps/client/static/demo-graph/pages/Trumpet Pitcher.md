*Sarracenia flava*, in the [[Sarraceniaceae]] family. Native to [[North America]].

```map
Virginia, USA @ 37.5, -78.8
North Carolina, USA @ 35, -77.8
South Carolina, USA @ 33.7, -80.5
Georgia, USA @ 31.5, -82.6
Florida, USA @ 30.3, -83
```

Tall yellow trumpets with a lid, which lure insects with nectar until they slip into the tube and cannot climb out. It is hardy, so it can live outdoors in a bog garden all year, and it needs a cold winter rest.

- Light: full sun
- Water: rainwater, standing in a tray of it all summer

See also [[Venus Flytrap]] and [[Tropical Pitcher Plant]].
