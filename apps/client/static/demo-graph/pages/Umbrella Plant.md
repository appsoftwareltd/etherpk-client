*Heptapleurum arboricola*, in the [[Araliaceae]] family. Native to [[China]].

Glossy leaflets arranged like the spokes of an umbrella. It is tough, grows into a small tree, and can be cut back hard when it does.

- Light: bright, indirect
- Water: when the top half of the pot is dry

See also [[Fatsia]].
