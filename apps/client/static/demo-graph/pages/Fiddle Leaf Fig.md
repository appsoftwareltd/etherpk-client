*Ficus lyrata*, in the [[Moraceae]] family. Native to [[West Africa]].

```map
Sierra Leone @ 8.6, -11.8
Liberia @ 6.5, -9.4
Côte d'Ivoire @ 7.5, -5.5
Ghana @ 7.9, -1
Southern Nigeria @ 6.5, 5.9
```

Huge violin-shaped leaves and a reputation for drama. Move it, let it get cold, or change its watering, and it drops leaves in protest. Find it a bright spot, leave it there, and it settles.

- Light: bright, with a little direct sun
- Water: when the top half of the pot is dry

See also [[Rubber Plant]] and [[Ficus Ginseng]].
