*Lactuca sativa*, in the [[Asteraceae]] family. A crop first grown in Egypt, from a wild lettuce of the [[Mediterranean]] and [[Western Asia]].

```map
Nile Delta, Egypt @ 30.8, 31
Anatolia, Turkey @ 39, 34
Western Syria @ 35, 36.4
Northern Iraq @ 36.2, 44
Northern Iran @ 36.5, 51.5
```

Quick to grow and quick to bolt in hot weather, so sow a short row every couple of weeks rather than a long one once. Cut-and-come-again leaves regrow several times. Slugs are the main threat (see [[Pests]]).

- Grow: sow outdoors from spring to late summer
- Harvest: weeks after sowing

See also [[Cabbage]].
