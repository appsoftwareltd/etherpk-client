*Dahlia pinnata*, in the [[Asteraceae]] family. Native to [[Mexico]].

```map
Hidalgo, Mexico @ 20.5, -98.9
Guanajuato, Mexico @ 21, -101.2
Michoacán, Mexico @ 19.2, -101.9
Puebla, Mexico @ 19, -97.9
```

The national flower of Mexico, grown from tubers and bred into thousands of forms, from tiny pompoms to dinner-plate blooms. In cold gardens lift the tubers in autumn and store them frost-free. Slugs adore the young shoots.

- Grow: plant tubers in spring
- Flowers: late summer to the first frost

See also [[Cosmos]] and [[Zinnia]].
