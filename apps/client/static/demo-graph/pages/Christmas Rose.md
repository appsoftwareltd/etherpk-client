*Helleborus niger*, in the [[Ranunculaceae]] family. Native to [[Europe]].

```map
Bavarian Alps, Germany @ 47.6, 11
Austrian Alps @ 47.2, 13
Dolomites, Italy @ 46.4, 11.8
Slovenia @ 46.1, 14.8
Gorski Kotar, Croatia @ 45.4, 14.8
```

Not a rose. It is a hellebore, with white flowers that open in the depths of winter, sometimes through snow. Like most of its family it is poisonous.

- Grow: part shade, under shrubs
- Flowers: winter

See also [[Snowdrop]] and [[Delphinium]].
