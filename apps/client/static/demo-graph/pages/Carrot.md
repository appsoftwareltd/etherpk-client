*Daucus carota*, in the [[Apiaceae]] family. Native to [[Central Asia]].

```map
Afghanistan @ 34.5, 67
Tajikistan @ 38.9, 70
Uzbekistan @ 41.3, 69.6
```

The first cultivated carrots were purple and yellow. Orange ones came out of Dutch breeding in the seventeenth century. Carrot fly finds the crop by smell, so thin the seedlings in the evening, and sow [[Chives]] or [[Onion]] nearby to confuse it (see [[Pests]]).

- Grow: sow outdoors thinly, in stone-free soil
- Harvest: from summer on

See also [[Parsley]] and [[Onion]].
