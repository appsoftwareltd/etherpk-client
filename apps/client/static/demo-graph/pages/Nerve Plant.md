*Fittonia albivenis*, in the [[Acanthaceae]] family. Native to [[South America]].

Small leaves with a net of white or pink veins. It faints when dry, as dramatically as a [[Peace Lily]], and recovers just as fast. It is a classic terrarium plant because it loves humid, still air.

- Light: medium, indirect
- Water: keep evenly moist

See also [[Polka Dot Plant]] and [[Peace Lily]].
