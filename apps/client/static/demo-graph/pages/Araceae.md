The arum family, or aroids. Their flowers sit on a fleshy spike, the spadix, wrapped in a hooded leaf, the spathe. On a [[Peace Lily]] the spathe is white and on an [[Anthurium]] it is red. Many grow on dim rainforest floors or climb tree trunks towards the light, which is why so many of them cope with life indoors. Most carry calcium oxalate crystals in their sap, so keep them away from pets that chew.
