*Tradescantia zebrina*, in the [[Commelinaceae]] family. Native to [[Mexico]] and [[Central America]].

Purple and silver striped leaves on stems that root wherever they touch soil. It grows fast and gets leggy, so pinch it back and push the cuttings into the same pot to keep it full.

- Light: bright, indirect. The colour fades in shade
- Water: when the top of the soil is dry

See also [[Golden Pothos]] and [[Swedish Ivy]].
