*Allium ursinum*, in the [[Amaryllidaceae]] family. Native to [[Europe]].

Carpets damp woods in spring with broad green leaves and white starry flowers, and the woods smell of garlic for weeks. If you forage it, crush a leaf to check for that smell: [[Lily of the Valley]] grows in the same places, looks similar, and is poisonous.

- Light: shade, outdoors
- Water: keep moist in spring

See also [[Chives]] and [[Lily of the Valley]].
