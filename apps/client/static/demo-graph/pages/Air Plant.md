*Tillandsia ionantha*, in the [[Bromeliaceae]] family. Native to [[Mexico]] and [[Central America]].

```map
Veracruz, Mexico @ 19.5, -96.9
Oaxaca, Mexico @ 17, -96.7
Chiapas, Mexico @ 16.6, -92.6
Guatemala @ 15.5, -90.3
Nicaragua @ 12.9, -85.2
```

A bromeliad with no need of soil. It takes water and food in through the fine scales on its leaves. Despite the name, it does not live on air: dunk it in water once a week and let it dry upside down within a few hours.

- Light: bright, indirect
- Water: soak for twenty minutes weekly, then dry

See also [[Spanish Moss]] and [[Urn Plant]].
