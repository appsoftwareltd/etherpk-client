*Adiantum raddianum*, in the [[Pteridaceae]] family. Native to [[South America]].

Delicate, fan-shaped leaflets on black, wiry stems. It is the most beautiful [[Fern]] on sale and the least forgiving: let it dry out once and it turns to a crisp. If that happens, cut it back to the base and keep it moist. It often regrows.

- Light: medium, indirect
- Water: never let it dry out

See also [[Boston Fern]].
