*Curio rowleyanus*, in the [[Asteraceae]] family. Native to [[Southern Africa]].

```map
Eastern Cape, South Africa @ -32.3, 26.4
```

Trailing strings of round, pea-sized leaves. It is a daisy relative, and the small white flowers smell of cinnamon. It dies from overwatering far more often than from thirst.

- Light: bright, with some direct sun
- Water: when the soil is dry

See also [[Burro's Tail]] and [[String of Hearts]].
