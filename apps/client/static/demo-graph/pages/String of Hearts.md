*Ceropegia woodii*, in the [[Apocynaceae]] family. Native to [[Southern Africa]].

```map
KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa @ -28.7, 30.9
Eswatini @ -26.5, 31.5
Mpumalanga, South Africa @ -25.6, 30.4
Limpopo, South Africa @ -23.9, 29.5
Zimbabwe @ -19, 29.8
```

Thread-thin trailing stems hung with small, heart-shaped, silver-marbled leaves. Small tubers form along the stems, and each one can grow a new plant.

- Light: bright, with some direct sun
- Water: when the soil is dry

See also [[String of Pearls]] and [[Hoya]].
