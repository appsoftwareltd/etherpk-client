*Clematis montana*, in the [[Ranunculaceae]] family. Native to the [[Himalaya]] and [[China]].

A vigorous climber covered in pink or white flowers in late spring. Clematis like their roots cool and their heads in the sun, so shade the base with a slab or another plant.

- Grow: roots shaded, top in sun
- Flowers: late spring

See also [[Honeysuckle]] and [[Delphinium]].
