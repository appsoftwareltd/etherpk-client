*Tropaeolum majus*, in the [[Tropaeolaceae]] family. Native to the [[Andes]].

```map
Colombia @ 4.6, -74.5
Ecuador @ -1.5, -78.5
Peru @ -9.5, -76.5
```

Round leaves and bright orange, red and yellow flowers, all edible with a peppery bite. Sown near brassicas it attracts the cabbage white caterpillars and blackfly away from the [[Cabbage]] (see [[Pests]]).

- Grow: sow outdoors in late spring, in poor soil
- Flowers: summer to the first frost

See also [[Cabbage]] and [[Pot Marigold]].
