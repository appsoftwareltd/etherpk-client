*Gymnocalycium mihanovichii*, in the [[Cactaceae]] family. Native to [[South America]].

```map
Paraguayan Chaco @ -21.5, -60.5
```

A bright red, orange or yellow ball perched on a green stem. The coloured top has no chlorophyll and cannot feed itself, so it is grafted onto a green cactus that does the feeding for it.

- Light: bright, indirect
- Water: when the soil is dry

See also [[Golden Barrel Cactus]].
