A family that looks thrown together at random: [[Asparagus]], the [[Snake Plant]], [[Agave]], [[Bluebell]] and [[Hosta]] are all members. Botanists grouped them by their DNA rather than their looks, and have moved several genera in and out as the evidence came in.

Every member in this graph links here, so **Backlinks** lists them all.
