*Chlorophytum comosum*, in the [[Asparagaceae]] family. Native to [[Southern Africa]].

Arching striped leaves, and long runners carrying baby plants that root the moment they touch soil. Every spider plant in the country descends from one somebody gave away. Brown leaf tips usually come from the fluoride and salts in tap water rather than anything you did.

- Light: bright, indirect
- Water: when the top half of the pot is dry

See also [[Snake Plant]] and [[Golden Pothos]].
