*Crocus vernus*, in the [[Iridaceae]] family. Native to [[Europe]].

Goblets of purple, white and yellow that open in the sun at the end of winter, often the first food of the year for bumblebees. Plant the corms in a lawn and they spread.

- Grow: plant corms in autumn
- Flowers: late winter to early spring

See also [[Snowdrop]].
