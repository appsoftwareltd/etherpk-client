*Aspidistra elatior*, in the [[Asparagaceae]] family. Native to [[Japan]].

```map
Osumi Islands, Japan @ 30.35, 130.53
```

The Victorian parlour plant. It survived gas lamps, coal smoke and gloomy hallways, which earned it the name. It grows very slowly, so buy one the size you want.

- Light: low. Direct sun bleaches the leaves
- Water: when the top half of the pot is dry

See also [[Kentia Palm]].
