*Echinocactus grusonii*, in the [[Cactaceae]] family. Native to [[Mexico]].

A perfect ball of golden spines. It is endangered in the wild, where dams flooded much of its habitat, yet common in garden centres. It grows very slowly.

- Light: full sun
- Water: when completely dry, and hardly at all in winter

See also [[Saguaro]] and [[Bunny Ears Cactus]].
