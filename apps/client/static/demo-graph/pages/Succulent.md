---
title: Succulent
aliases:
  - Succulents
---
A succulent is any plant that stores water in thick leaves, stems or roots, so it can go weeks without rain. It is a way of living rather than a family: the [[Cactaceae]], the [[Crassulaceae]] and the [[Aloe Vera]] got there separately. Every cactus is a succulent, though not every succulent is a cactus.

They die of kindness more often than neglect. Use gritty, free-draining compost (see [[Soil]]), water thoroughly only once the pot is completely dry, and hardly at all in winter (see [[Watering]]). Most want as much light as you can give them.
