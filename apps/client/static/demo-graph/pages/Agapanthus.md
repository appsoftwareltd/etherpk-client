*Agapanthus africanus*, in the [[Amaryllidaceae]] family. Native to [[Southern Africa]].

```map
Cape Peninsula, South Africa @ -33.96, 18.4
Swellendam, South Africa @ -34.02, 20.44
```

Round heads of blue or white flowers on tall stems, above strappy leaves. It flowers best when its roots are crammed into a pot, so do not rush to repot it.

- Grow: full sun, in a tight pot
- Flowers: late summer

See also [[Amaryllis]].
