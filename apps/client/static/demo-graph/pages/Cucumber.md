*Cucumis sativus*, in the [[Cucurbitaceae]] family. Native to [[India]].

A climbing plant with tendrils. Greenhouse varieties produce fruit without pollination, and if pollinated they turn bitter, so grow them away from outdoor types. Keep the air humid and the roots moist.

- Grow: sow indoors in spring, in warmth
- Harvest: often, while the fruit is young

See also [[Courgette]] and [[Borage]].
