*Salvia rosmarinus*, in the [[Lamiaceae]] family. Native to the [[Mediterranean]].

A woody, evergreen [[Herb]] from dry Mediterranean hillsides. Cold does not kill it as often as wet does: give it sharp drainage and a sunny spot and it lives for years. Botanists recently moved it into the sage genus.

- Light: full sun
- Water: rarely, once established

See also [[Sage]], [[Thyme]] and [[Lavender]].
