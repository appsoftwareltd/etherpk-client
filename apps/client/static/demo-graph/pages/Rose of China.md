*Hibiscus rosa-sinensis*, in the [[Malvaceae]] family. Cultivated for so long that nobody is sure where it began, probably [[Southeast Asia]] or the [[Pacific Islands]].

Big, papery flowers in red, pink, orange or yellow, each lasting a day or two. Indoors it needs a sunny window and steady watering, or the buds drop before they open.

- Light: as bright as possible
- Water: keep evenly moist in summer

See also [[Money Tree]].
