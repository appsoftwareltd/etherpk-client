*Coriandrum sativum*, in the [[Apiaceae]] family. Native to the [[Mediterranean]] and [[Western Asia]].

Leaves for curries and salsas, and seeds that are a spice in their own right. It runs to seed quickly in heat or dry soil, so sow a little every few weeks. To some people it tastes of soap, which is down to their genes.

- Light: sun, with some afternoon shade in summer
- Water: keep moist, or it bolts

See also [[Parsley]] and [[Dill]].
