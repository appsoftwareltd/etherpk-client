*Betula pendula*, in the [[Betulaceae]] family. Native to [[Europe]].

```map
Scottish Highlands @ 57.3, -4.5
Southern Sweden @ 57, 14.8
Finland @ 62.5, 25
Thuringia, Germany @ 50.9, 11
Poland @ 52, 19.4
```

A light, graceful tree with white bark and leaves that turn butter yellow in autumn. It casts a light shade that plants can grow under, and it is one of the first trees to colonise open ground.

- Grow: full sun
- Colour: autumn

See also [[English Oak]].
