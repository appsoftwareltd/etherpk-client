*Lonicera periclymenum*, in the [[Caprifoliaceae]] family. Native to [[Europe]].

```map
Southern England @ 51.15, -1.2
Ireland @ 53.2, -8
Central France @ 46.8, 1.7
Netherlands @ 52.2, 5.6
Southern Sweden @ 57, 14.8
```

A native climber whose scent is strongest at dusk, when it calls in the moths that pollinate it. Birds eat the red berries in autumn.

- Grow: roots in shade, head in sun
- Flowers: summer

See also [[Common Jasmine]] and [[Clematis]].
