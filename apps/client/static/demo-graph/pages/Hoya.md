*Hoya carnosa*, in the [[Apocynaceae]] family. Native to [[China]] and [[Australia]].

Thick waxy leaves on trailing stems, and clusters of star-shaped flowers that look carved from wax and smell sweet at night. Do not cut off the old flower stalks: the next flowers come from the same place.

- Light: bright, indirect. More light means more flowers
- Water: when the pot is nearly dry

See also [[Sweetheart Hoya]] and [[String of Hearts]].
