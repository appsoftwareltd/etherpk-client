*Dendrobium nobile*, in the [[Orchidaceae]] family. Native to the [[Himalaya]] and [[China]].

Cane-like stems that flower along their length in spring. It needs a cool, dry rest in winter to set flower buds. Keep it warm and wet all year and you get leaves and little else.

- Light: bright, with some morning sun
- Water: often in summer, hardly at all in winter

See also [[Moth Orchid]] and [[Cymbidium]].
