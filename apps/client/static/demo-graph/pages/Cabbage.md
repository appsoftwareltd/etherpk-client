*Brassica oleracea Capitata Group*, in the [[Brassicaceae]] family. Native to [[Europe]].

Wild cabbage grows on the sea cliffs of western Europe, and from that one species gardeners bred cabbage, [[Kale]], [[Broccoli]], cauliflower, Brussels sprouts and kohlrabi. Net it, or cabbage white caterpillars strip it to the veins (see [[Pests]]).

- Grow: sow in spring, plant out firmly
- Harvest: summer, autumn or winter, depending on the variety

See also [[Kale]], [[Broccoli]] and [[Nasturtium]].
