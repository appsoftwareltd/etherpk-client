*Philodendron hederaceum*, in the [[Araceae]] family. Native to [[Central America]] and [[South America]].

```map
Costa Rica @ 9.9, -84.1
Panama @ 8.6, -80.1
Colombia @ 4.6, -74.5
Venezuela @ 7.5, -66
Pará, Brazil @ -4.5, -52.5
```

Heart-shaped leaves on long trailing stems. It is often mistaken for [[Golden Pothos]]. The philodendron has thinner, softer leaves, and each new one unfurls from a papery sheath. It is one of the few plants that genuinely copes with a dim room.

- Light: anything from bright to quite dim
- Water: when the top half of the pot is dry

See also [[Golden Pothos]] and [[Monstera]].
