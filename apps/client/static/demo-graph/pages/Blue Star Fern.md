*Phlebodium aureum*, in the [[Polypodiaceae]] family. Native to the [[Caribbean]] and [[South America]].

```map
Cuba @ 22.1, -79.6
Dominican Republic @ 18.8, -70.5
Puerto Rico @ 18.25, -66.45
Venezuela @ 7.5, -66
Colombia @ 4.6, -74.5
```

Deeply lobed fronds in an unusual blue-green, growing from furry rhizomes that creep over the surface of the pot. It is tougher than most ferns and copes with drier air.

- Light: medium, indirect
- Water: when the top of the soil is dry

See also [[Staghorn Fern]] and [[Rabbit's Foot Fern]].
