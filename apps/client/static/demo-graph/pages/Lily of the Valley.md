*Convallaria majalis*, in the [[Asparagaceae]] family. Native to [[Europe]].

```map
Southern England @ 51.15, -1.2
Central France @ 46.8, 1.7
Thuringia, Germany @ 50.9, 11
Poland @ 52, 19.4
Southern Sweden @ 57, 14.8
```

Tiny, scented white bells hanging from arching stems in late spring. It spreads slowly in shade. Every part is poisonous, and its leaves are easily confused with those of [[Wild Garlic]].

- Grow: shade, moist soil
- Flowers: late spring

See also [[Wild Garlic]] and [[Hosta]].
