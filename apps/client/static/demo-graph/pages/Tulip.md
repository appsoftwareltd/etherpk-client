*Tulipa gesneriana*, in the [[Liliaceae]] family. Native to [[Central Asia]].

```map
Almaty, Kazakhstan @ 43.2, 76.9
Kyrgyzstan @ 41.5, 74.6
Uzbekistan @ 41.3, 69.6
Tajikistan @ 38.9, 70
```

Wild tulips come from the mountains of Central Asia, and Ottoman gardeners bred them long before the Dutch. In the 1630s tulip bulbs in the Netherlands briefly sold for extraordinary prices. Plant a [[Bulb]] late in autumn, deeper than you think.

- Grow: plant bulbs late in autumn
- Flowers: spring

- Keukenhof, near Lisse, plants about seven million bulbs each autumn and opens for eight weeks in spring [[Where to Learn About Plants]]
  ```map
  Keukenhof @ 52.26987, 4.54726
  ```
