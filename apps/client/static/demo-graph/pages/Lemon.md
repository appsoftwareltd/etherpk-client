*Citrus × limon*, in the [[Rutaceae]] family. A hybrid, probably first grown in [[India]].

```map
Assam, India @ 26.2, 92.9
Meghalaya, India @ 25.5, 91.4
```

An evergreen tree with scented flowers and fruit that takes most of a year to ripen, so plants often carry both at once. Grow it in a pot that spends summer outside and winter in a cool, bright room.

- Grow: in a pot, with citrus feed in summer
- Harvest: when fully yellow

See also [[Curry Leaf]] and [[Olive]].
