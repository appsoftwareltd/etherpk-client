*Pisum sativum*, in the [[Fabaceae]] family. Native to the [[Mediterranean]] and [[Western Asia]].

Nodules on its roots hold bacteria that take nitrogen from the air, so peas feed the [[Soil]] for whatever comes after. Fresh peas turn starchy within hours of picking, which is why frozen peas often taste sweeter than shop-bought fresh ones. Gregor Mendel worked out the basics of genetics by breeding peas.

- Grow: sow outdoors from early spring, with twiggy sticks to climb
- Harvest: when the pods are full

See also [[Runner Bean]] and [[Sweet Pea]].
