*Rhipsalis baccifera*, in the [[Cactaceae]] family. Native to [[South America]] and [[Madagascar]].

```map
Venezuela @ 7.5, -66
Bolivia @ -17, -64.7
Bahia, Brazil @ -12.5, -41.7
Toamasina, Madagascar @ -18.15, 49.2
```

Pencil-thin, trailing green stems with no spines to speak of. It is the only cactus that grows wild outside the Americas, in tropical Africa, Madagascar and Sri Lanka too, probably carried there by birds eating its berries.

- Light: bright, indirect
- Water: when the top of the soil is dry

See also [[Fishbone Cactus]].
