*Rubus idaeus*, in the [[Rosaceae]] family. Native to [[Europe]].

```map
Scottish Highlands @ 57.3, -4.5
Southern Norway @ 60.5, 8.5
Finland @ 62.5, 25
Thuringia, Germany @ 50.9, 11
Poland @ 52, 19.4
```

Canes that fruit in summer or in autumn, and the two are pruned differently: cut summer types back after they fruit, and autumn types to the ground in late winter. Mix them and you get the best of both.

- Grow: plant canes in winter, with a support
- Harvest: summer or autumn, depending on the type

See also [[Strawberry]] and [[Blueberry]].
