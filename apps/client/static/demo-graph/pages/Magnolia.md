*Magnolia grandiflora*, in the [[Magnoliaceae]] family. Native to [[North America]].

An evergreen tree with leathery leaves and huge, lemon-scented white flowers. Magnolias evolved before bees existed, and their flowers are pollinated by beetles.

- Grow: full sun, sheltered wall
- Flowers: late summer

See also [[Camellia]].
